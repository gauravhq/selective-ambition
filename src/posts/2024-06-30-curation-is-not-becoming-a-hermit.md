---
title: Curation Is Not Becoming a Hermit
date: 2024-06-30
pageDescription: "Choosing what enters your attention on purpose is not the same as shutting the world out."
---
At some point I realized I did not need to see everything. I needed to see the right things, for me, specifically, right now. That distinction sounds small when you say it out loud, but it changed how I thought about my own attention.

Curation gets a bad reputation. People hear the word and assume it means cutting yourself off, avoiding the news, refusing to know what is happening in the world. That is not what this is. Curation does not mean avoiding information. It does not mean becoming a hermit.

It means getting intentional about what actually gets to enter your head. Not everything that could reach you today. Not everything that wants your attention. Just the things that are genuinely useful to you, in your specific life, at this specific point in it.

That is a much smaller ask than cutting yourself off from the world entirely, and it does not require any dramatic gesture to start. It just requires admitting that most of what reaches you was never actually chosen. It arrived because nobody was guarding the door, and you assumed that was simply how it had to be.
