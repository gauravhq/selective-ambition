---
For years I assumed the problem was me.

I thought I was not busy enough, not optimised enough, that I needed a better system, a better morning routine, a healthier relationship with my calendar. Every time I felt overwhelmed and unsatisfied, I looked for the flaw in myself first, because that felt like the responsible thing to do.

It took a long time to consider the other possibility. That the system itself was the problem. That a current running underneath all of it, pulling everyone in the same direction, was what was making people sick, not their personal habits.

The problem was never really my discipline or my time management. It was the culture around me, the shared set of unwritten rules everyone followed without ever agreeing to the terms out loud.

Most people do not wake up one day and choose to perform busyness for an audience. They wake up inside a system that rewards exactly that and quietly punishes anyone who tries the alternative. Then they do what any reasonable person would do next. They play along.
