---
title: Is This Mine to Carry
date: 2023-08-27
pageDescription: "One belief changes the question from should I do this to is this mine to carry."
---
There is a belief I keep coming back to, the closest thing I have to a JOMO identity in a single sentence. I am someone who chooses what to carry. I am not someone who carries everything just because I can.

That is not a productivity trick. It is not a lifestyle brand. It is just a belief about what kind of person you are.

Once you hold it clearly, decisions get simpler. Not easy, simpler. The question changes from "should I do this?" to "is this mine to carry?" The second question is much easier to answer, because you already know what is on your list. You wrote it down. You checked it. You know.

The people who seem most at peace with their choices are not the ones who made perfect decisions. Nobody makes perfect decisions. They are the ones who know what they are choosing and why they are choosing it. They own the choice. The choice does not own them. That is the whole difference, and it is available to anyone willing to sit down and actually look at their list.
