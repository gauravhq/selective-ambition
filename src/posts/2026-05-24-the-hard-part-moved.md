---
title: "The Hard Part Moved"
date: 2026-05-24
pageDescription: "The hard part used to be building. Now the hard part is choosing what to build."
---
Andrew Ng said it more cleanly than I ever managed to. The hard part used to be figuring out how to build things, he said. Now the hard part is figuring out what to build.

I read that line and felt something click into place. That is exactly the thing nobody had warned me about. For years I assumed that once I could build, the hard part would be over. I would finally know what to do with all those ideas sitting on my list, because the only thing stopping me had been the building itself.

It did not work out that way. The moment building stopped being the obstacle, choosing became one. And choosing, it turned out, was the part I was worst at. Give me a wall to push against and I will push at it for years. Give me an open door and every other door standing open next to it, and I will not know which one to walk through first.

The question had simply moved houses. It did not go away. It just stopped being about ability and started being about judgment, which is a much harder thing to practice.
