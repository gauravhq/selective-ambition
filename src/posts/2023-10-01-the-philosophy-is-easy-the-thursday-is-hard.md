---
title: The Philosophy Is Easy, the Thursday Is Hard
date: 2023-10-01
pageDescription: "Accepting you cannot do everything is easy in theory and hard on an actual Thursday."
---
Oliver Burkeman wrote an entire book about accepting that you cannot do everything. It's called Four Thousand Weeks, and he is right. You cannot do everything. Nobody can. The math was never in your favor to begin with.

What I want to add is smaller and less philosophical. It is what that acceptance actually feels like when you are sitting at your desk on a Thursday afternoon and the list is still long and none of it feels optional. The philosophy is easy to agree with. The Thursday is hard to live through.

I have not mastered this balance, and I am not sure anyone does. Some weeks the public scoreboard wins outright. I stay late for something that matters to my career but does not matter to me, not really, and I do it anyway because that is what the week requires.

That is the 5% shift applied to the other 95%. You do what you have to do, because a job and a mortgage and other people's deadlines are real. And then, with whatever is left over, you do what you actually want to do.
