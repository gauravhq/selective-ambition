---
title: "This Was Partly a Letter to Myself"
date: 2026-04-19
pageDescription: "Writing about slowing down turned out to be a way of asking myself the same questions."
---
I have been avoiding saying this directly, but here it is. This was partly a letter to myself.

I started writing because I had questions, the same ones you probably have. How do you stop chasing everything without it feeling like giving up. How do you say no to things that look good on paper but feel heavy in your chest the moment you agree to them. I did not have tidy answers when I started. I am not sure I have tidy answers now.

I wrote the first pages from my desk, at ten at night, on a Tuesday, because that was the only quiet hour I had. The questions were live then. They are still live now. I still catch myself mid chase, still feel the pull toward one more thing.

That is exactly why I trust the answers I did find. They did not come from someone standing safely outside the problem, looking down at it with a clean explanation. They came from someone still inside it, working it out one messy page at a time, the same way you might be working it out right now.
