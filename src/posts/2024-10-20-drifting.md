Some of your commitments were never actually chosen. They just accumulated.

This is the quadrant where resonance is low and intentionality is low. You do not especially care about these things, and you did not deliberately decide to take them on either. They arrived by accident and stayed by inertia.

The committee you joined because someone asked and saying no felt awkward in the moment. The app subscription you have never gotten around to canceling. The project you volunteered for in year one and simply forgot to leave, so it kept renewing itself on your calendar without asking your permission again.

Most people carry more of these than they realize, and that is exactly what makes them easy to miss. They do not feel like problems. They feel normal, the way something can sit in a room long enough that you stop noticing it is there.

Noticing them is the first step, and it is smaller than it sounds. Ask, of each thing on your list, whether you would choose it again today if it were not already there. If the honest answer is no, you have found one worth a closer look.
