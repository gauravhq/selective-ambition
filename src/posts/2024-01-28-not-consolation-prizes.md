---
title: Not Consolation Prizes
date: 2024-01-28
pageDescription: "The small, ordinary pleasures were never a backup plan but the point all along."
---
These quiet things are not consolation prizes for missing out on something bigger. They are the main event. We just forgot that.

We got so busy chasing the extraordinary, the achievement worth mentioning, the story worth telling, that we stopped noticing the ordinary was already good. The coffee was already good before we started tasting it. The kitchen was already warm before we noticed the warmth. The evening was already enough before we decided it needed to be more than that.

We were not missing anything. We just were not paying attention, because our attention was going somewhere else, somewhere louder, somewhere that seemed more likely to matter at the time.

That is the quiet, slightly embarrassing truth underneath a lot of this. Nothing had to be added to make these moments count. Nothing had to be built, launched, or shared to make them real. The good part was already there, sitting quietly under all the noise we kept piling on top of it, waiting for us to notice it instead of scrolling past it toward whatever we thought we were supposed to be doing instead.
