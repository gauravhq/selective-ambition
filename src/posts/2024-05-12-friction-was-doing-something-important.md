---
title: Friction Was Doing Something Important
date: 2024-05-12
pageDescription: "Choosing a person over an AI tool that is always available reveals what friction protects."
---
I almost stayed at the desk that night. I almost chose the AI over the person waiting in the kitchen. And the AI would have been perfectly happy to have me stay.

That is the thing about it. It does not mind either way. It is always available, any hour of the night, and it makes no difference to it. It does not need you the way a person does. It does not get lonely when you leave, and it will not notice if you never come back at all.

That is the beauty of it, honestly. No guilt, no waiting, no disappointed look across a kitchen. Just a tool doing exactly what you ask, exactly when you ask it, with zero emotional cost attached.

That is also the trap. AI and the new tools built around it removed a kind of friction that used to sit between you and staying at your desk forever. And friction, it turns out, was doing something important the whole time. It was the thing that occasionally made you get up, walk into another room, and remember there were people in your life who would not wait forever.
