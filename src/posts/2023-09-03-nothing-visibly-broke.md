title: Nothing Visibly Broke
date: 2023-09-03
pageDescription: "Saying a clean no rarely blows anything up, but you may never know for sure."

Afterward I replayed it more times than I would like to admit. I could not tell if the manager was annoyed, or indifferent, or quietly filing me away as someone not to ask again. His face had done that small reset, like a screen refreshing, and then the meeting just moved on to timelines.

He never brought it up again. Not once. I am honestly not sure whether that silence means it was fine, or whether it cost me something I cannot see from where I am standing. Nothing visibly broke. That much I know.

But "nothing visibly broke" is not quite the same thing as "the sky stayed up." It might be closer to this: the sky did whatever the sky does, on its own schedule, and at some point I simply stopped checking the forecast every hour.

That is the part that surprised me most. Clarity is rare enough that people tend to respect it, even the ones who do not agree with it, even when a small part of them would have preferred a softer, longer, more apologetic version of no.
