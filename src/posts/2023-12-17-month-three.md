---
title: Month Three
date: 2023-12-17
pageDescription: "The real problems in any project only show up once the easy solutions run out."
---
The first week of any project is fun. Everything is new. You are sketching ideas, imagining what it could become, and it is all potential with no friction yet. Everyone can do week one. Week one asks nothing hard of you.

The third month is different. By then the obvious solutions have already been tried. What is left are the real problems, the ones that require actual thinking, the ones without a tutorial waiting online to solve them for you. You sit at your desk and stare at the screen and wrestle with something until your brain finally makes a connection it had not made before.

That is where the craft actually is. That is where the satisfaction lives. That is the point where the work stops being something you are doing and starts being something you are making, something with your own fingerprints on it instead of a template's.

Most people never get there. They are already starting something new in month two, chasing the next week one, because week one feels so much better than the middle of anything. Staying is the only way in.
