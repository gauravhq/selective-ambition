---
title: "Seven Types of Rest"
date: 2023-04-02
pageDescription: "Sleep is only one of seven kinds of rest your mind and body actually need."
---
Rest is not one thing, and treating it like one thing is probably why yours is not working.

Dr. Saundra Dalton-Smith, in her book Sacred Rest, identified seven distinct types the body and mind need, physical, mental, emotional, social, sensory, creative, and spiritual. Most of us collapse all of that into a single idea, sleep, and stop there. But you can sleep a full eight hours and still wake up mentally exhausted, emotionally depleted, and socially drained, because none of those needs substitutes for the others.

Physical rest is not the same as emotional rest. Scrolling alone in bed can quiet your body while leaving your social tank completely empty. Creative rest, taking in beauty instead of producing it, is its own category, separate from the mental rest of switching your brain off a problem.

Most working professionals are running a deficit in at least four of these seven types at once, which is why more sleep never fixes the tiredness. I had been treating rest as a single thing and failing at it for years. Once I saw the full list, the failing finally made sense, because I had only ever been resting one part of myself.
