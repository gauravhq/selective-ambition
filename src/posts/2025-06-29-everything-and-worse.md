---
title: Everything I Needed, and Worse Than Before
date: 2025-06-29
pageDescription: "Having every tool you once wanted can somehow leave you feeling worse."
---
The question did not arrive dramatically. No music, no meaningful pause. It simply surfaced, quietly, the way something does once it has been waiting for an empty enough moment to be noticed.

I have everything I need to build anything I want. Everything. So why do I feel worse than when I could not.

I did not answer it right away. I just let it sit there, which is harder than it sounds when you are used to answering everything quickly and moving on. It was not a rhetorical question dressed up to sound clever. I genuinely did not know the answer.

Underneath it sat a low, slow sadness behind my ribs, the kind that has been there long enough that you stop noticing it day to day. It only surfaces when something forces you to be still for a minute, which nothing in my schedule had ever been built to allow.

More capability was supposed to mean more relief eventually. That was the whole promise, implied in every tool I had picked up along the way. Instead the tools arrived, the capability arrived, and the feeling underneath it all somehow got heavier instead of lighter.
