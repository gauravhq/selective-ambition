---
title: The Enough-Day Check
date: 2023-07-23
pageDescription: "A one-question nightly check for whether an ordinary day was actually enough."
---
At the end of the day, ask yourself one question: was today enough? Not extraordinary. Not productive. Not impressive enough to mention to anyone. Just enough. Enough conversation, enough rest, enough of the ordinary moments that made up the day.

If the answer is yes, do not rush past it looking for the catch. Notice it. Let it be good, without adding a but at the end. You are allowed a day that was simply enough, with nothing left over to prove.

An enough day is not a failed day. It is easy to treat it that way, as if only the extraordinary days count, but that is not true. An enough day is not a consolation prize. It is the whole point.

This is a nightly check, not a life review. You are not asking whether the year is going well or whether you are becoming who you hoped to be. Just today. Was today enough. Ask it plainly, answer it honestly, and let the answer stand.
