---
title: "No Is a Short Word That Takes a Long Time to Say"
date: 2024-12-22
pageDescription: "A four-sentence decline took three weeks to write and only thirty seconds to send."
---
Marcus emailed me directly about a cross-team data migration. The invite was generous. He said he had specifically asked for me, that the project would have real visibility with senior leadership, and that the team was small and sharp. Reading it, I felt the pull of being wanted.

I sat on the email for four days without answering it.

Then I spent almost three weeks drafting a four-sentence decline. I kept typing the word unfortunately and deleting it again, because nothing about the situation was actually unfortunate. It was a choice, and writing it down as a choice felt too honest to put in an email.

The version I finally sent was simple. I thanked him, said I was focused on something else right now, wished the team well, and hit send before I could reread it a fifth time.

About ten minutes later, relief arrived. I felt physically lighter, as if something had let go in my chest.

That moment took three weeks to reach. The decision itself, in the end, took thirty seconds to make.
