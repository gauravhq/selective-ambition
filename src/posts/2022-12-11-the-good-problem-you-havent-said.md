---
title: "The Good Problem You Haven't Said Out Loud"
date: 2022-12-11
pageDescription: "A good problem is still a real stress once you say it out loud."
---
Some stress never gets talked about, because saying it out loud feels ungrateful. The job everyone congratulated you for, that is quietly wearing you down. The exciting opportunity that is eating your weekends. The busy season you are proud of and exhausted by at the same time. On paper these look like wins. Out loud, admitting they are hard can feel like bragging with a complaint stapled to it.

So you don't say it. You just carry it, telling yourself it's a good problem, which is another way of saying it does not count as a real one. But your body does not sort stress by whether it deserves sympathy. It only registers weight, and weight adds up whether or not anyone is allowed to hear about it.

Is there something like that in your life right now? A strain you have filed under lucky to have and never actually spoken out loud? Try saying it to one person, plainly, without the apology attached. Notice whether it feels lighter simply for having left your mouth. Stress that stays unspoken does not disappear on its own. It stays in the body, waiting to be named.
