---
title: The Shelf Practice
date: 2023-05-28
pageDescription: "A notebook habit for getting ideas out of your head and onto a shelf."
---
Get a notebook. An actual physical one, not another app that will end up back on the list you are trying to escape.

Write down every idea, every project, every "I should really do this" that has been sitting in your head taking up space. Give each one a paragraph. What it actually is. Why it excites you. What the first real step would be if you started tomorrow. Then close the notebook and put it on a shelf, somewhere you will see it but will not open every day.

The shelf is not the bin. Nothing you write down is being thrown away or judged as a bad idea. It is being respected enough to wait for a season when you can actually give it your attention, instead of competing for space with everything else in your head right now.

The point is not the notebook itself. The point is getting these ideas out of your head and into a place where they stop quietly generating guilt every time you notice them. Some of them will not matter anymore when you finally come back to that shelf. That is fine too. That is what the shelf is for.
