---
title: The 2 A.M. Brain
date: 2025-09-28
pageDescription: "Waking at 2 a.m. with your mind already working is a kind of night shift."
---
You are asleep. Then you are not. Nothing woke you, not a noise, not a light, not a dream you can point to. The list did.

The projects. The emails you have not answered. The thing you forgot to do yesterday. The thing you did today but are not sure you did right. The thing you told someone you would start by now and have not touched. None of it arrives politely, one at a time. It arrives all together, at 2 a.m., in the dark, with nothing around to distract you from any of it.

Your body was resting. It had earned that much. You went to bed, closed your eyes, did everything a person is supposed to do to fall asleep. Your brain did not get the memo. It kept working quietly in the background, the way a laptop keeps its fan running long after you have closed the lid.

Call it the night shift. Nobody scheduled it and nobody is paying you for it. It just started happening somewhere along the way, and now it happens most nights, and you wake up tired in a way that has nothing to do with how long you actually slept.
