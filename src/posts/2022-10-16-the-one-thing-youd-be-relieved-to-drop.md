---
title: "The One Thing You'd Be Relieved to Drop"
date: 2022-10-16
pageDescription: "There is a difference between what you should drop and what you'd feel relieved to drop."
---
Here is the big question, the one worth sitting with longer than the others. If you chose to miss out on one thing this week, just one, what would it be?

Not the thing you know you should give up, the kind that shows up on every list of good advice. That version of the question is easy to answer and just as easy to ignore, because "should" rarely moves anyone for long.

Ask it differently. What is the one thing that, if it disappeared from your week, would leave you relieved instead of guilty? There is a real difference between those two. Should points at a standard someone else set for you. Relieved points at something your own body already knows, if you let yourself listen.

Once you have that thing named, ask the second half of the question. What is actually stopping you from dropping it, fear of what someone will think, a habit so old you forgot it was a choice, a story about who you are supposed to be. You do not have to act on the answer today. Just let yourself see it clearly.
