---
title: Is Easier the Same as Better
date: 2024-04-21
pageDescription: "AI never argues or grows tired, but that ease is not the same as being better."
---
AI does not judge you. It does not argue with you. It does not need anything from you in return. It just responds, instantly, patiently, endlessly, for as long as you want to keep typing.

Real people are the opposite of all that. Slow. Unpredictable. Sometimes boring, occasionally annoying, prone to interrupting you at the worst possible moment. And that is precisely what makes them irreplaceable, even when it does not feel that way at the time.

The kid who interrupts your work to show you a bug crawling across the floor, or a leaf that happens to look like a face. That interruption costs you something. It is also the kind of moment you actually remember years later, long after the work itself is forgotten.

The partner who gets upset because you have been on the laptop all week. That is not really about the laptop. It is her saying "I miss you" in the only way she has left to say it, and a chatbot will never say those words and mean them.

The question is not whether AI is easier to talk to. It is. The real question is whether easier is the same as better.
