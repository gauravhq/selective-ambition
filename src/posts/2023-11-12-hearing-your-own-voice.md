---
title: Hearing Your Own Voice
date: 2023-11-12
pageDescription: "It took months of steady writing, not occasional bursts, before the sentences finally sounded like his own."
---
I had written on and off for years, the way a lot of people do. A post here, a draft there, never for long enough to get past the surface of it.

Then I stopped starting new hobbies and just wrote. For months, with nothing else competing for the same hours.

The first two months felt like shouting into a well. The sentences were stiff. The ideas felt borrowed from somewhere else, dressed up to look like mine.

Around month four, something shifted. I started hearing my own voice in the writing. Not a polished voice. Not even a confident one. Just a recognizable one, the kind where you read a paragraph back and think, that sounds like me and nobody else.

That recognition only came from repetition. From doing the same thing long enough for the awkwardness to burn off and leave something real underneath it.

If I had kept splitting my time between writing and whatever else I was dabbling in, I would never have reached month four. The dabbling would have kept me permanently at month one, where everything is new and nothing is yours yet, forever starting and never arriving.
