---
title: "The Friday I Chose the Spreadsheet Over the Sun"
date: 2026-03-08
pageDescription: "A finished Friday afternoon got spent on a spreadsheet nobody asked for instead of outside."
---
By three thirty on a Friday, I was done. Inbox cleared, every deliverable sent, nothing left on the list. I could have closed the laptop and walked outside into that warm, late-afternoon light that makes everything look inviting.

Instead I spent the next two hours making a spreadsheet nobody had asked for presentation ready. Rearranging columns. Adding colour to the headers. Making it look finished for a meeting that had not even been scheduled yet.

At 5:45 I sent it with a note. "Got ahead on this, wanted to share early." The reply came back a minute later. "Great, thanks." It was probably never opened again.

I had stayed visible until quarter to six on a Friday, and somehow that mattered to me more than the walk would have, more than the sun through the window I kept glancing at while I worked. I chose the spreadsheet over the afternoon, and the strange part is that I did not even experience it as a choice while I was making it. It just felt like the obviously correct thing to do with two free hours. It was not.
