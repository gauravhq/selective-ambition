---
title: The Guilt Got Bored and Left
date: 2024-02-18
pageDescription: "Guilt shows up the moment you rest on purpose, but it leaves if you simply wait it out."
---
There was a Saturday with nothing planned. I mean actually nothing, not "nothing planned but I will probably work on something." I lay down on the living room carpet and stared at the ceiling.

For the first twenty minutes it felt wrong, like I was wasting time that someone else, somewhere, was using better. The guilt sat on my chest like a cat I had not invited, heavy and impossible to ignore. It whispered the usual things. You are behind. Everyone else is working. You should be too.

I stayed on the carpet anyway.

And after a while, the guilt got bored and left. It always does, if you wait long enough. It does not storm out. It just quietly loses interest once it realizes you are not going to argue with it or obey it.

The trick is not arguing the guilt down with a good enough reason to rest. The trick is waiting. Outlasting it. Letting it sit there being loud and boring until it gets tired of talking to someone who has stopped listening, and finally leaves you alone with the ceiling and the quiet.
