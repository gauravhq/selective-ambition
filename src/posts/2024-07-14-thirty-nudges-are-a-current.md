---
title: Thirty Nudges Are a Current
date: 2024-07-14
pageDescription: "None of your daily inputs are large on their own, but together they form a current."
---
None of the inputs pulling at you today are big enough, on their own, to explain how tired you feel by evening. A notification here. A scroll there. A newsletter subject line you did not even open. Each one is small enough to dismiss individually, and that is exactly why you keep dismissing them.

But thirty tiny nudges in the same direction stop being thirty tiny nudges. They become a current. You do not notice a current by looking at any single wave. You notice it by realizing you have drifted somewhere you did not choose to go.

You are swimming in that current all day, every day, and then wondering, at nine at night, why you feel wrung out when you technically did not do very much. The size of any one input was never the point. The direction all thirty were pulling in is the point.

Once you see it as a current instead of a pile of small, forgivable things, the question changes. Not "was this one bad." But "which way has today actually been pulling me."

That question is worth asking before the day ends, not after.
