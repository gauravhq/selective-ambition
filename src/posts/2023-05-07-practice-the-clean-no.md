---
title: Practice the Clean No
date: 2023-05-07
pageDescription: "A monthly practice of saying no without a reason attached to it."
---
Once a month, on purpose, practice the clean no. Pick something that genuinely deserves a no, not a maybe dressed up as a request.

Say it without a reason attached. Skip "I'm not doing that because" and the paragraph of defense that usually follows it. Say only "I'm not doing that." Full stop. No explanation. No apology tucked in afterward to soften it. No follow-up listing the benefits you might be missing, just to prove you thought it through. The reasons are yours. They do not owe anyone an audit.

Here is what actually happens next, almost every time. The world does not end. The person on the other end is mildly surprised for about two seconds, and then they move on, because most people are far less invested in your justification than your guilt assumes they are. And you, meanwhile, have just made room for something that actually matters, simply by refusing to spend your energy defending a no that was already true before you said it.

It does not stop being uncomfortable the first time. But each time you practice it, it gets a little less terrifying, and a little more like something you can just do.
