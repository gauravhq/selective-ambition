---
title: The Guilt Pile
date: 2024-06-16
pageDescription: "Eleven unread newsletters can sit in an inbox like guilt long after you stop reading them."
---
I left two Slack channels and unsubscribed from eleven newsletters in the same week. I kept exactly one. The one I actually read. The one that, when it landed in my inbox, I opened with something other than dread.

The other eleven had been sitting there for months, unread and undeleted, a small guilt pile taking up space in my head just by existing. I never opened them, but I always knew they were there, quietly sitting in a folder like unfinished business.

Unsubscribing felt strangely dramatic for something so small. Like I was closing a door on information I might need someday. But the important stuff, it turns out, has a way of finding you through other channels regardless of how many newsletters you delete. It always has.

I have not missed anything. Not one deadline, not one insight, not one tool everyone else supposedly knew about. The guilt pile is gone, and nothing bad moved in to replace it. Just some quiet, and an inbox that finally matches what I actually read instead of what I once meant to.
