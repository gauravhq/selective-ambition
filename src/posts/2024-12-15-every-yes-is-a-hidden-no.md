---
title: "Every Yes Is a Hidden No"
date: 2024-12-15
pageDescription: "Every yes you give is also a quiet no to something that never gets named."
---
Every yes has a hidden cost. Every yes is also a no to something else, and you rarely see the second half of that trade because the something else never gets a name.

You said yes to the extra committee. That meant no to two evenings a week that used to be yours. You did not notice the trade because the committee had a name on a calendar and the evenings did not.

You said yes to the side project. That meant no to weekends with family. The project had a deadline and a group of people counting on you. The weekends just quietly disappeared.

You said yes to your own ambition, to the idea that you should always be doing more. That meant no to sleep, to rest, to the quiet space where you used to think clearly. Ambition had a voice. Sleep did not.

None of this means you should stop saying yes. It means noticing what you are actually agreeing to give up, before the trade gets made without you.
