---
title: "The Two-Axis Map"
date: 2024-10-27
pageDescription: "Two simple axes show whether a commitment was truly chosen or just accumulated by default."
---
Here is a simple way to see where any commitment in your life actually sits. Picture two axes.

The first is resonance: does this genuinely matter to you, or does it just look like it should? The second is intentionality: did you choose this on purpose, or did it accumulate by default, one small yes at a time?

Put those together and you get four places a commitment can live. Something you drifted into without caring about it. Something you chose only for how it looks. Something good that found you by accident. And something you chose on purpose because it actually matters, which is the only quadrant worth aiming for.

The goal is not to empty the other three completely. That is not realistic, and chasing it will only make you miserable in a new way. The goal is the ratio. If most of your energy is going toward drifting and performing, you are working hard in exactly the wrong direction.

Draw your own map honestly. Put things where they actually belong, not where you wish they belonged. The point is not to feel bad about what you see. It is to see clearly enough to start moving something.
