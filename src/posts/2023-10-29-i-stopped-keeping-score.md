---
title: "I Stopped Keeping Score by Other People's Rules"
date: 2023-10-29
pageDescription: "A quiet, tired shift away from comparing your progress to everyone else's."
---
It sounds bold when I say it out loud. Like a line-in-the-sand moment, a morning I woke up and decided to stop caring about how I measured up. That is not how it happened.

It happened slowly, messily, over months of noticing the same pattern and getting tired of it. The pattern was simple and it repeated like a loop I could not find the off switch for. Accomplish something. Feel good for a minute. Open the phone. See someone who did something bigger, or faster, or younger than me. Feel bad. Close the phone. Go back to work. Do it again.

I did not have a breakthrough. I did not read the right article or hear the right podcast and suddenly see the light. I just got tired. Tired of feeling good for ninety seconds and bad for the rest of the day. Tired of a scoreboard I never agreed to play by.

That is the real starting point, if you are looking for one. Not clarity. Not enlightenment. Just exhaustion with a pattern that never once made me feel like enough.
