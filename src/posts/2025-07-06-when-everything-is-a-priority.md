---
title: When Everything Is a Priority
date: 2025-07-06
pageDescription: "A color coded task list stops working the moment every item turns red."
---
My task list used colour coding. Red for urgent. Orange for important. Yellow for get to it this week. Green for whenever.

That Tuesday morning I looked at the board and noticed something. Everything on it was red or orange. Twelve items, every single one urgent or important, according to a system I had built myself specifically to tell the difference between the two.

The colour coding that was supposed to help me prioritise had quietly stopped working, because when everything is a priority, nothing actually is one anymore. The whole point of the system was contrast, and there was none left to find. Just a wall of red and orange tiles, each carrying a deadline, each carrying a name that was supposed to mean something important.

The screen looked almost angry at me, twelve small accusations in a neat row. I scrolled through slowly and felt my jaw tighten with every item, which should have told me something on its own, long before I read a single task description closely.

Twelve urgent things cannot all be the most urgent thing at once. A list like that has stopped being a priority list. It is just a list now.
