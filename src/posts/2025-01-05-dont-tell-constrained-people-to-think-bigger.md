---
title: "Don't Tell Constrained People to Think Bigger"
date: 2025-01-05
pageDescription: "People with real constraints do not need bigger dreams, they need a little more time."
---
The most common mistake in advice aimed at people with real constraints is telling them to dream bigger, think bigger, as if the problem is a lack of vision.

It rarely is. The person working two jobs does not lack vision. She lacks hours. The person on a work visa does not lack ambition. He lacks options, because his right to stay depends on an employer he cannot afford to challenge. Telling either of them to get clear on what they want misses the point.

They already know. They have known for a while. They want one evening that is not spoken for. One morning without a list waiting on the counter. One hour that belongs to them and not to the job, the commute, or the machine that runs the rest of their week.

That is not a small want dressed up as a big one. It is the actual want, stripped of everything that does not fit inside their week. The advice that tells them to think bigger is not wrong because it demands too much. It is wrong because it aims at a problem they do not have, while ignoring the one they carry every day.
