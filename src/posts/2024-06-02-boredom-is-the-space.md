---
title: Boredom Is the Space Your Ideas Live In
date: 2024-06-02
pageDescription: "A quiet afternoon with no inputs let a small, useful idea finally get a word in."
---
On a day with no inputs at all, around four in the afternoon, doing nothing in particular with a cup of tea, an idea showed up. Not a big one. Just a small connection about a project I had been circling for weeks without seeing clearly.

I grabbed the nearest piece of paper, which happened to be an electricity bill, and wrote it down before it slipped away. Two sentences, maybe. But those two sentences changed the direction of the next week's work in a way none of my usual planning had managed to.

That thought would not have shown up on a normal day. On a normal day, that exact space in my head would have been filled already, by a podcast, a scroll, a notification demanding a glance. There was simply no room for it to arrive.

Boredom is not the enemy here, even though it gets treated like one. Boredom is the space where your own ideas finally get a word in, instead of being talked over by everyone else's. They are still in there, waiting. They just need a quiet room to speak up in.
