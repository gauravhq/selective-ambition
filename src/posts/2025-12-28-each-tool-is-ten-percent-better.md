---
title: "Each Tool Is 10 Percent Better, Together They Cost Everything"
date: 2025-12-28
pageDescription: "Each tool alone saves time, but stacked together they quietly consume all your attention."
---
You are not just doing more things these days. You are managing more things, supervising more things, switching context more often, carrying more open loops in your head than you would like to admit.

Each new tool arrives with the same quiet promise: this will make things easier. And narrowly, it usually keeps that promise. The dashboard is faster than the spreadsheet. The new platform does save you a step. Taken one at a time, none of it is a bad decision.

But nothing in your life arrives one at a time anymore. Add up enough tools that are each ten percent better than doing the thing by hand, and you can end up with a setup that together consumes nearly all of your attention just to keep running. Too many dashboards open. Too many notifications competing for the same few minutes. Too many systems that each save you time individually while quietly costing you focus collectively.

That particular kind of tired did not exist five years ago, not at this scale. It is not laziness, and it is not a discipline problem. It is arithmetic. Ten percent, ten percent, ten percent, until the total is everything you have.
