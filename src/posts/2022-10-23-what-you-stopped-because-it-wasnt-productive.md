---
title: "What You Stopped Because It Wasn't Productive"
date: 2022-10-23
pageDescription: "Something you once did for fun got dropped for not being productive enough."
---
Think of something you used to enjoy, purely for its own sake, that you quietly stopped doing because it did not produce anything. Reading for fun, before it became something to optimize or track. Cooking, before every recipe had to teach you a skill or feed a plan. Walking without a podcast in your ears, just walking. Drawing badly, with no intention of getting better, just because moving a pencil felt good.

At some point, one by one, these things got weighed against a productivity standard they were never trying to meet, and they lost. Not because you decided reading for pleasure was worthless. It simply stopped feeling like a good use of time compared to everything else competing for the same hour.

So which one was yours? Try to remember the exact moment you noticed you were not doing it anymore, or if there even was a moment, or if it just faded without you noticing.

Would you want to start again? Not to get good at it, not to turn it into a habit tracker entry, just to do the thing itself, badly or slowly, the way you used to before it needed to justify itself.
