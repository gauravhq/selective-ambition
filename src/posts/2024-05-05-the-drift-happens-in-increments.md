---
title: The Drift Happens in Increments
date: 2024-05-05
pageDescription: "Nobody decides in one clear moment to trade people for a screen instead."
---
Nobody decides, in one clear moment, to trade a relationship for a screen. It happens in increments so small that each one is basically invisible while it is happening.

The first time I brought the laptop to the couch after dinner, just to check one thing, was three years ago. That is not the whole story though, because there was a second time. The second time was easier than the first. The tenth time had already become a habit I did not think twice about. By the fiftieth time, the laptop on the couch was simply the default, and not having it there would have felt strange.

I was not choosing the laptop over my wife. I want to be precise about that, because it matters. I was just choosing the laptop, each time, for its own small reason. The "over my wife" part was a consequence I never actually saw happen.

Consequences that arrive slowly do not announce themselves. They do not knock. They just accumulate quietly in the background until one day the total is impossible to ignore, and by then it looks sudden even though it never was.
