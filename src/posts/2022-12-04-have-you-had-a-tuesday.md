---
title: Have You Had a Tuesday
date: 2022-12-04
pageDescription: "A quiet Tuesday is often when you notice you have more but feel worse."
---
It rarely happens on a big day. Not the day you get promoted, or the day everything falls apart at once. It happens on an ordinary Tuesday, in the middle of a perfectly normal week, when nothing dramatic is going on and somehow that is exactly why you notice it.

You look up from whatever you are doing, the meetings, the errands, the endless small tasks, and a thought arrives uninvited: why do I feel worse now than when I had less? You have more of everything, more responsibility, more options, more things to show for yourself, and less of whatever it was that used to make an ordinary day feel fine.

That is the Tuesday realization. It is quiet, it does not announce itself, and it is easy to brush past because nothing forced it. Nobody yelled. Nothing broke.

Have you had one of your own? Try to remember exactly where you were. What were you doing when the thought landed? What had you added to your life recently that, without meaning to, had started taking more than it gave back?
