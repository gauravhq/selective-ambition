---
title: "You Can't Build a Life Alone"
date: 2024-03-17
pageDescription: "Real ambition cannot be built alone, and the people around you are why it matters."
---
You can build genuinely amazing things alone, especially now, with tools that never get tired and never ask for anything back. That part is real, and it is worth saying clearly instead of pretending otherwise.

But you cannot build a life alone. Not a real one. The people around you are not a distraction pulling you away from your ambitions. They are the reason those ambitions mean anything to you in the first place. Take them out of the picture and see how much of your ambition still feels worth chasing.

Choosing the slow, messy, inefficient friction of an actual human conversation over the smooth comfort of a screen will rarely feel efficient in the moment. It will cost you time you could have spent building something. It will not always go well. Some nights you will do everything right and still feel like a ghost sitting in the chair anyway.

Choose it anyway. It might be the most important JOMO choice you will ever make, the one that decides, years from now, whether the life you built was actually worth having built in the first place.
