---
title: The Day I Unfollowed Thirty Accounts
date: 2024-06-23
pageDescription: "Unfollowing had nothing to do with liking people less and everything to do with feeling behind."
---
Liking these people had nothing to do with it. That was the strange part. I unfollowed thirty accounts in one sitting, and almost every one belonged to someone I genuinely liked. Their content was just making me feel behind.

There was a developer I had followed for two years, always shipping, always posting clean screenshots of something new working. I liked the guy. But every post left a small deflation behind, like a phone battery quietly draining in the background.

There was a career coach whose "harsh truths" threads I used to find motivating. At some point they started feeling like being yelled at by a stranger on the internet for not working hard enough. Which, looking at it honestly, is exactly what it was.

The hardest one was a college friend, doing well, posting a new trip or milestone often. I felt guilty unfollowing, like it said something about the friendship. It did not. It was a comment on what I could handle seeing at 6:52am, in bed, before my brain had any defenses up.

None of the catastrophes I feared actually happened.
