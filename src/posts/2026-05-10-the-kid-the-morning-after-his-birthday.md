---
title: "The Kid the Morning After His Birthday"
date: 2026-05-10
pageDescription: "A kid who opens every gift at once ends up too full to enjoy any of them."
---
Picture a kid the morning after his birthday. A mountain of wrapped gifts still on the table from the night before. He tears open the first one. Amazing. Tears open the second. Even better. By the fifth or sixth, he is not really looking at what is inside anymore. The ripping has become the rush, not the toy underneath the paper.

By the end of the day he is sitting in the middle of scattered wrapping paper and half played with toys, looking a little lost. Not sad exactly. Just full. Too full to enjoy any one thing properly.

A week later, most of those toys sit untouched in a corner of the room. He stopped playing with them on day two. But he will not let anyone throw them out. They are his. He wanted them, all of them, and that wanting turned out to be a completely different thing from enjoying any single one.

That was me, except the wrapping paper was half-built apps instead of action figures, and the mountain of gifts was a list of ideas I had finally been given the power to open, all at once, on the same overwhelming morning.
