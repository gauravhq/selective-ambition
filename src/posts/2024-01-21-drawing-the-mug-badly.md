---
title: Drawing the Mug Badly
date: 2024-01-21
pageDescription: "Drawing badly turned out to matter more than drawing well, because nobody was waiting on it."
---
The things that came back when I left some gaps in my days are small. They will not make anyone's highlight reel.

A conversation where I was actually there, heard what the other person said, and laughed at something dumb without checking my phone. The laugh was real.

Reading a novel again, just for pleasure, no purpose beyond the story itself. It felt like finding an old friend I had not realized I missed.

And drawing, badly. I bought a cheap sketchbook and drew the blue mug with the chipped handle. It came out looking like a mug drawn by someone who had heard a mug described to them but had never actually seen one. I showed my wife. She tilted her head and asked, "Is that a vase?" We stood there laughing until the laughter started feeding itself, well past the point the joke deserved.

Drawing is the only thing I do where quality does not matter and nobody is waiting for the output. Just a pencil, paper, and the quiet, useless struggle of trying to make my hand draw what my eyes actually see.
