---
title: Delete the App, Not the Account
date: 2024-06-09
pageDescription: "Removing a single app from a phone, not the whole account, quietly changed a daily habit."
---
I deleted a social app from my phone. Not the account. I can still reach it on my laptop whenever I actually want to look something up. But the phone was the real problem, not the platform itself.

The phone was where the mindless checking happened. The 45-second scroll while waiting for coffee. The "just a quick look" that somehow turned into twenty minutes I could never quite account for afterward. None of that required the account to exist. It only required the app to be one thumb tap away.

Removing it did not change my life in any dramatic way. I want to be honest about that. But it removed roughly a dozen tiny triggers from my day, a dozen small moments where I would have felt a flicker of falling behind for no real reason at all.

A dozen fewer triggers turned out to be a dozen more minutes of actual quiet. It is a small trade on paper. I would make it again without hesitating.
