---
title: "Noticing Is the Thing"
date: 2026-04-12
pageDescription: "Noticing a habit will not fix it, but it finally gives you a choice about it."
---
I still open too many tabs. I still catch myself comparing my week to strangers on the internet who seem to be doing more of everything with less effort. I still feel the old pull toward one more project, one more idea, one more thing I could be doing right now instead of resting.

The difference is that I notice it now.

Noticing does not solve anything by itself. It will not close the tabs for you or quiet the comparison. But it does something quieter and, it turns out, more useful. It gives you a choice you did not have before.

Before you notice, you are just running. You do not experience the tab opening or the comparison starting as a decision, because it does not feel like one. It feels automatic, like breathing. You are three tabs in before you even register that you opened the first one.

After you notice, you can actually decide. Keep running, if that is genuinely what you want. Or stop, just for a moment, and see what is left when the running pauses. Noticing is not the fix. It is the doorway to one.
