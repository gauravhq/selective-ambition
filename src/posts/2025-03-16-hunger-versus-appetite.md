---
title: "Hunger Versus Appetite"
date: 2025-03-16
pageDescription: "Hunger is a specific want you can name, appetite is a vague one borrowed from everywhere."
---
There is a difference between hunger and appetite, and learning to feel the gap between them matters.

Hunger is specific. You know exactly what you want. You can describe it in a sentence, feel it somewhere in your body, and it has nothing to do with what anyone else thinks of it. It is just yours, plain and clear.

Appetite is vague. You want "more." You want "success." You want "to build things." Big, confident-sounding words that fall apart the moment someone asks what they actually mean on a Wednesday afternoon. You cannot point to the thing itself, because the wanting was never really coming from you. It arrived from everywhere else, absorbed rather than chosen.

Appetite is a crowd, loud and vague, everyone talking at once. Hunger is a single voice you can actually hear once the crowd quiets down.

FOMO lives on appetite. It feeds on "more" and "everyone else is doing it" and words too big to hold onto. The other side of it starts the moment you get honest about your actual hunger, the specific, unglamorous thing you would still want on a quiet Wednesday with no one watching.
