---
title: Each Input Is a Tiny FOMO Trigger
date: 2024-07-21
pageDescription: "Three small moments on an ordinary day show how tiny inputs quietly plant the feeling of falling behind."
---
There was a reel from a productivity creator, ring light and all, showing his "5 AM deep work routine" while the sun came up behind him. I watched it over toast at 7:15, still not dressed. It did not inspire me. It made me feel late, like the day had already started without me elsewhere.

There was a newsletter with the subject line "The 7 AI tools your competitors are already using." I never opened it. I did not need to. In three seconds it planted a small seed of being behind, and that seed sat with me through two meetings I was supposed to be paying attention to.

There was a Slack message near ten at night, a coworker's commit message reading "finally cracked the caching problem." I saw it while brushing my teeth. I was not working. But seeing someone else still solving problems at that hour made me feel like I had quit too early, like I had left the game while it was still being played.

None of these ruin a day by themselves. That is exactly the problem.
