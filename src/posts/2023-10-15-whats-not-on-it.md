---
title: "What's Not on It"
date: 2023-10-15
pageDescription: "Some goals only feel like yours because everyone around you has them."
---
Notice what is not on my scoreboard. "VP by 40." Not there. "Build a company." Not there. "Get 10,000 followers." Not there. "Be recognised in my industry." Not there.

Those are not bad goals. Plenty of people want them and mean it and build good lives around them. They are just not mine. I spent years assuming they were, mostly because I never stopped to check.

They are goals I absorbed from the water I was swimming in. From posts I scrolled past at midnight. From articles that told me what ambitious people my age were supposed to want. From conversations where someone mentioned a title or a milestone and I quietly filed it away as something I should want too, without ever asking why.

For years, those borrowed goals looked exactly like my own. I could not have told you the difference. I said them out loud in performance reviews and half believed myself. They were not mine. Writing my actual list is what finally showed me the difference, and it took longer than I expected to admit that.
