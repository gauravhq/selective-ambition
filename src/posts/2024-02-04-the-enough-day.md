---
title: The Enough Day
date: 2024-02-04
pageDescription: "Nothing remarkable happened on this ordinary day, and that turned out to be exactly the point."
---
I woke at 6:15, made coffee, and stood by the window drinking it slowly. The commute was the same commute it always is, radio instead of a podcast, someone talking about the weather. At work I did my job. No breakthroughs. I solved a small problem that had been bothering me for two days, and felt a minor satisfaction that faded by lunch.

I came home and we made dinner together. She did the rice, I did the vegetables. We ate at the table, not the couch. She told me an uninteresting story about her day, and I told her one back, and both got heard all the way through.

I washed the dishes. I read a novel about a lighthouse keeper for about an hour. I got sleepy around 9:30.

Lying in bed, a thought landed with quiet certainty, the kind that does not argue its way in but simply arrives fully formed. Today was good.

Nobody would make a movie about it. Nobody would post about it. It was just an enough day, and that turned out to be everything.
