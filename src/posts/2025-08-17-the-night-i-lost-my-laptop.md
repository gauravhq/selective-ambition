---
title: The Night I Lost My Laptop
date: 2025-08-17
pageDescription: "Losing the option to work for one evening taught me more than rest ever had."
---
I left my laptop at work by accident. Did not notice until I was already home, my hand reaching for a bag that felt lighter than it should have.

The first ten minutes were awful. My hands kept reaching for a laptop that was not there. I checked my phone, put it down, picked it up again. I paced the apartment like a dog that heard a doorbell somewhere it could not locate.

Then, about twenty minutes in, something loosened. My shoulders dropped. Actually dropped, as if they had been sitting up near my ears for days without my noticing. I put on a genuinely terrible movie and enjoyed every minute of it, because for once I was not splitting my attention between the screen and six other things at once.

The next morning I felt more rested than I had in weeks. Not because I had done anything differently. Because the choice to work had been taken out of my hands entirely, and it turned out that was the only way I was actually going to put it down.

Sometimes the best thing that can happen to you is losing the option to work, even just for one night.
