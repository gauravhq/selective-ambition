---
title: The Cross-It-Off Practice
date: 2023-06-04
pageDescription: "A five-minute experiment in crossing something off on purpose, not from finishing it."
---
Here is a five-minute experiment you can run today. Go to your real to-do list, the actual one, not a hypothetical version of it in your head. Pick one thing on it. Cross it off. Not because you did it. Because you decided not to.

That is the whole exercise. You are choosing to miss out, on purpose, with full intention, instead of letting the item quietly expire from neglect the way most abandoned tasks do. This is different. This is a decision, made in the open, with your own hand on the pen.

Now notice how it feels. If it feels like relief, some tightness loosening for no reason you can fully explain, you now know what this whole practice is actually about. It is not about doing less for the sake of doing less. It is about the relief of choosing.

If instead it feels terrifying, that is useful information too. It means you just found the one thing gripping you hardest, the item your identity had quietly attached itself to. Either way, you learned something in under five minutes that most to-do lists never tell you.
