---
title: "Wanting to Have a Podcast"
date: 2025-03-30
pageDescription: "Wanting to have something and wanting to actually do the work of it are not the same."
---
I ran my own list through the nobody-knew test, and the first answer that came back stung a little.

I had been carrying a podcast idea for over a year, with a name, a format, even a guest list. When I asked myself if I would still want it if no one ever knew, the answer arrived fast. No.

I did not want to record a podcast. I wanted to have a podcast. I wanted to be the person introduced at events as someone who also hosts a podcast about something. The bio line, not the show. The actual work, the recording, the editing, the scheduling, publishing every week, none of that excited me at all.

The same thing turned up with an analytics dashboard that had been sitting on my list so long it felt like a debt I owed myself. I did not want to build it. I wanted to be the kind of person who builds things like that.

Once I saw it plainly, the guilt over never starting either one disappeared almost immediately, like setting down a coat I had not noticed I was still wearing.
