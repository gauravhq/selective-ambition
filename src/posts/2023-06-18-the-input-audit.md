---
title: The Input Audit
date: 2023-06-18
pageDescription: "A simple audit for cutting the apps and feeds that only add noise."
---
Make a list of every app, channel, newsletter, dashboard, and notification that demands your attention on a regular day. All of it, even the ones you barely notice anymore.

Then go through the list one item at a time and ask the same question of each: does this help me do what actually matters, or does it just make me feel like I should be doing more? The first kind earns its place and stays. The second kind is just noise wearing the costume of usefulness, and it goes.

Most of us are carrying at least ten inputs that add nothing but that low hum of "you are behind." You will not notice how loud it is until some of it is gone.

"My count was fourteen. I turned off eight of them in one evening. The next morning was noticeably quieter, and I didn't miss a single one." That is the usual result. Nothing falls apart. No one calls asking where you went. Run the audit today and see what your real number is.
