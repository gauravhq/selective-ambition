---
title: "Then AI Happened"
date: 2026-05-31
pageDescription: "New tools finally made it possible to build something imagined for three years in a single evening."
---
Then AI happened. Not the vague, someday version people had been talking about for a decade. The real kind. The kind you could actually use, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and the rest.

One day I sat down, described what I wanted to build, and the thing started building itself. Not perfectly. Not without work on my part. But it was happening, in front of me, on a normal evening.

The first time I got something small working, nothing fancy, just a working tool, I sat and stared at the screen. I had been carrying that particular idea for three years. Three years of it living only in a document and in my head. And now it existed, after one evening.

I do not know a clean way to describe that feeling. It is like a door you have been leaning your whole weight against for years, and one morning it just swings open. You stumble through it because you are still pushing. And on the other side is everything you had been imagining, except now it is real enough to click on.

The wall was gone. That is really the whole story.
