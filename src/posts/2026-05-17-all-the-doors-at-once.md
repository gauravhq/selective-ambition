---
title: "Every Door at Once"
date: 2026-05-17
pageDescription: "Every idea became possible at the same time, and that felt very different from choosing one."
---
When the wall came down, I did not walk through one door. I walked through all of them, at the same time.

I started the app. Then the website. Then a side project, and then another side project, and then the someday thing I had been putting off for years because I finally could not think of a reason not to start it.

What I actually needed was to pick one door and stay in that room for a while. What I had instead was every door open at once, and me running between all of them like I could occupy each room if I just moved fast enough.

For about six months, it felt incredible. Every idea I had ever written down was suddenly within reach, and I was reaching for every single one. There was a kind of high in that, the feeling of finally being able to do the thing after years of only imagining it.

Then the excitement got a roommate. Nobody invited it. It just moved in one day and stayed. That roommate was stress, and it did not leave when the excitement eventually did.
