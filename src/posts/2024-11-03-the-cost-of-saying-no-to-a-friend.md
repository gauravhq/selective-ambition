---
title: "The Cost of Saying No to a Friend"
date: 2024-11-03
pageDescription: "Saying no to a friend's big idea can cost the friendship even when the reasons are sound."
---
My friend Dev came to me once with an idea for a documentation tool, excited in a way I had rarely seen him. He wanted a fifty-fifty co-builder, and he wanted it to be me.

Part of the pull toward saying yes was his excitement. More of it was that I wanted to keep being the person Dev trusted with his biggest ideas.

I said no anyway. Saying it felt like telling him I did not believe in him, even though that was never what I meant. I did not have the hours. He said he understood, and technically he did.

But something changed after that. The texts thinned. The weekend hikes stopped, not all at once, more like a tide going out.

Dev launched the tool six months later with someone else.

I do not think he was angry. I think he was hurt in a way neither of us knew how to talk about, and feelings do not follow logic that cleanly. They just change the temperature of a friendship by a degree or two.

Saying no has a real cost. I do not pretend otherwise. I just decided, that time, it was still the right choice.
