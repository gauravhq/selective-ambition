---
title: "Step Off the Treadmill"
date: 2025-10-12
pageDescription: "The only way to stop feeling behind on an endless treadmill is to step off."
---
Picture a treadmill with no finish line and no speed that counts as fast enough. You run, you glance sideways, someone else is running faster, so you speed up. You glance again. Someone else is faster still. The belt just keeps going. It was never built to end.

The only way to stop feeling behind on a machine like that is to step off it entirely.

The afternoon I finally did, it was not dramatic. I had a cable to return to a shop down the road, so I went for a walk. No podcast, no phone, nothing to fill the space. Halfway there, I noticed I was thinking about the project I cared about, the one that kept pulling me back even when I was not trying to think about it. For the first time in weeks it felt exciting again instead of heavy. Relief, plain and physical.

The counting did not stop that day. I caught myself at it again the next morning, doing the same tired arithmetic over someone else's post. But the urge was a little quieter after that walk. Not gone. Just quieter. Some days that is the only win available to you.
