---
title: The Event Planner
date: 2023-09-24
pageDescription: "An event planner traded prestige for smaller weddings and finally started sleeping again."
---
Picture an event planner who spent years chasing exactly what everyone else in her industry chased. Bigger events. Corporate galas, product launches, the kind of work that looks impressive on a portfolio.

Then she noticed what she actually wanted was smaller. Not another gala, not more prestige. She wanted to plan small weddings for people she liked. Fewer clients. Less money. Way less prestige than before.

Her old colleagues think she downgraded. Maybe by their measure she did. But she sleeps through the night now, for the first time in a decade, and that is not nothing. She still sometimes sees their corporate events on Instagram and feels a pang of something she cannot quite name. Not regret exactly. Just something.

Different scoreboard, that is all it is. It is easy to hear a story like this and assume it is about ambition versus laziness. It is not. She did not stop working hard. She just stopped measuring her hours by a scoreboard that was never actually hers, and started keeping one that fit the life she was actually living.
