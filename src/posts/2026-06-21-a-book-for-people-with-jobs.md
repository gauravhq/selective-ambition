---
title: "A Book for People With Jobs"
date: 2026-06-21
pageDescription: "This is for people with real jobs and real bills, not people who already have it figured out."
---
This is for people with actual jobs, actual bills, and actual ambitions, and not enough hours for any of them. People who check email before breakfast. People who think about work in the shower. People who feel a flicker of guilt on a free Saturday because they are not being productive.

I know the feeling from the inside. I once spent an entire Sunday afternoon reorganizing my task management system instead of doing any of the tasks inside it. I moved things into new folders, renamed projects, colour coded priorities. I felt productive the whole time. I was not. I had just found a new way to avoid the list instead of working through it.

If that sounds familiar, you are in the right place. Nothing here assumes you can drop everything and disappear to think. It assumes you still have to show up tomorrow, with the same job and the same responsibilities, and it tries to work inside that.

If you have already found your balance and your peace with ambition, you do not need this. Go enjoy it. This is for the rest of us, still figuring it out on a weekday.
