---
title: Guilty for Being Stressed
date: 2025-09-07
pageDescription: "Feeling guilty about stress from good problems only pushes the stress into hiding."
---
Then comes the final twist, the one that keeps the whole thing quiet. You feel guilty for being stressed, because your particular stress comes from good problems. Options. Tools. Opportunities other people would trade for without hesitation.

So who are you to complain. Who are you to feel overwhelmed when, on paper, your life is more than fine. Someone out there, probably a lot of someones, would love to have exactly what is stressing you out now.

That thought does not make the stress disappear. It just adds a second layer on top of the first one. Now you are stressed, and you feel bad about being stressed, and somehow the second feeling is worse than the original, because at least the stress itself was honest.

This guilt is the lock on the cage. It is the reason you do not bring it up at dinner, do not mention it to a friend, do not ask anyone for help. Saying it out loud sounds like complaining about a life other people would want. And in a world that keeps reminding you how lucky you are, admitting you are struggling can feel like the most ungrateful sentence you could say.
