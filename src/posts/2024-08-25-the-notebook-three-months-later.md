---
title: "The Notebook, Three Months Later"
date: 2024-08-25
pageDescription: "Opening a notebook of shelved ideas three months later showed which ones were actually still alive."
---
Three months after writing "not now" nine times, I opened the notebook again, the way you check a grade you are not sure you want to see.

Three of the ideas I genuinely could not remember being excited about. They had felt urgent in November. By February they read like someone else's ideas entirely.

Three more still seemed decent on the page, but they did not pull at me the way they used to. They were interesting the way a magazine article is interesting, worth a read.

Two of them, the writing course and the analytics dashboard, still felt alive. I wrote a note next to each: still good, revisit later.

And then there was the podcast idea about unfinished projects, which made me laugh out loud, because by then I was three months into actually finishing something, and the joke of hosting a show about people who don't was no longer lost on me.

The list that had felt like nine doors closing back in September now felt like nine things I had simply set down for a while. Some were lighter than I remembered carrying. A few I had almost forgotten I was carrying at all.
