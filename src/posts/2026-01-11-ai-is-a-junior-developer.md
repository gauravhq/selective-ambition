---
title: "Treat AI Like a Junior Developer"
date: 2026-01-11
pageDescription: "Expecting AI to finish instantly just because it started instantly is the real problem."
---
Most of the frustration eases the moment you stop expecting the AI to be finished just because it says so.

Treat it like a junior developer instead. Not an insult, just an accurate job title. The kind of junior who tells you it is done and means it compiles. The kind who needs a code review every single time, not from carelessness, but because that is what junior means. Nobody expects a new hire to ship flawless work on the first attempt. You budget for the back and forth. You expect a review cycle. You build the correction into the plan before you even start.

With AI, we quietly drop that expectation, for one strange reason: speed. Because the draft appeared in ninety seconds instead of two days, some part of the brain assumes it is finished too. It never is. The speed of the first draft has nothing to do with the distance still left to travel.

Once you stop expecting perfection on delivery and start expecting a first pass that needs your eyes on it, the whole relationship gets calmer. You are not being failed. You are doing the review a junior always needs.
