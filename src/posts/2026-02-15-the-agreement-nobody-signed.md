---
title: "The Agreement Nobody Signed"
date: 2026-02-15
pageDescription: "Nobody wrote the rule that being overwhelmed proves you matter, but everyone follows it anyway."
---
There is an agreement nobody actually signed, and almost everybody quietly follows anyway.

If you are not overwhelmed, you are not trying hard enough. If your plate is not full, you probably are not that important. If you had a genuinely quiet weekend, something in your life must be wrong, and people will ask about it with real concern in their voice.

Nobody wrote this down. There was no meeting where it got approved. It built up slowly, through a thousand small signals that each seemed harmless on their own. The colleague who gets praised for staying late. The friend who gets quiet respect for hustling through a weekend. The four in the morning post that goes viral because someone wanted the world to know they were already awake and working.

Each signal by itself is nothing. Stacked together over years, they become the air in the room, so constant that you stop noticing you are breathing it. You just absorb the rule and start living by it, the same way everyone around you seems to be living by it, without either of you ever agreeing to the terms out loud.
