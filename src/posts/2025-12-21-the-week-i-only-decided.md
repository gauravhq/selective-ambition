---
title: "The Week I Only Decided"
date: 2025-12-21
pageDescription: "A week spent only planning and comparing options left nothing actually built by Friday."
---
I had a week last year where I did not build a single thing. I only decided about things, which felt like progress at the time and did not feel like anything by Friday.

Monday I reorganised my project list. Moved cards around a board for an hour, colour-coded them, added due dates I already knew I would change by Wednesday. Tuesday I ran the same prompt through four different AI tools just to compare the answers. Three hours gone. I picked none of them. Wednesday I laid out three approaches to the same idea, drew up pros and cons, texted a friend to ask what he thought. He replied, "just pick one." I felt annoyed, because he clearly did not understand the complexity. He was right, though. Thursday I built colour-coded calendar blocks for the week ahead. Forty five minutes on a calendar that changed nothing real.

Friday I looked back and saw it clearly. Five days spent managing my ambitions. Zero days spent acting on any of them. Busy every single day. Nothing to show for it.

The buffet ate the meal. I had piled my plate with planning and left no room for the food.
