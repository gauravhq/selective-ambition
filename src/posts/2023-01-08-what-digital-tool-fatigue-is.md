---
title: "What Digital Tool Fatigue Is"
date: 2023-01-08
pageDescription: "Digital tool fatigue is the hidden cost of too many apps each demanding attention."
---
Digital tool fatigue is what happens when the apps that were supposed to help you start quietly draining you instead.

Each one arrives with a clean promise. It will save you time, keep you organized, or make some part of your work easier. Taken alone, most of them even deliver on that promise, at least a little. The problem shows up only once you add them together. Ten small efficiencies do not simply add up to one big efficiency. They add up to ten separate things demanding a notification, a login, or a decision, all day long.

That adds up to a tax on your attention, and like most taxes, it is easy to ignore until you actually look at the total. You do not usually notice digital tool fatigue while it is happening. You notice it afterward, in how tired you feel despite having sat at a desk all day doing work that should not have been this exhausting.

The fix is rarely a better app. It is usually fewer of them. Turn a few off, even the useful ones, and the tax becomes visible by its absence.
