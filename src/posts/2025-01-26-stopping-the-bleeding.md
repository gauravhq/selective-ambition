---
title: "Stopping the Bleeding"
date: 2025-01-26
pageDescription: "You cannot escape every constraint, but you can stop adding to the ones that already exist."
---
Living with real constraints does not mean you are stuck waiting for them to disappear. It means directing your attention to the part of the situation you can actually do something about right now.

Start by looking honestly at everything on your plate. Some of it has to be there. The job, the bills, the people who depend on you. That part is not up for negotiation, and pretending otherwise just wastes energy you do not have to spare.

But some of what is on that pile, you put there yourself, without quite noticing. A commitment you drifted into. A habit dressed up as a requirement. Separate those out from the rest, and you will usually find more of them than you expected.

Then go slowly. Put back only the things you actually chose, one at a time, and see how the pile feels with a little less in it.

This is not about redesigning your entire life or waiting for circumstances to finally get easier. It is about stopping the bleeding on the half of the pile that was always yours to control, and letting that be enough for now.
