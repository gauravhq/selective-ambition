---
title: The Rohan Call
date: 2023-12-24
pageDescription: "A hard question from an old friend cuts closer than any sympathy from a wide network."
---
A Friday in March, I found out by email that I did not get a lateral move I had wanted. Short, polite, forgettable. That specific kind of small that comes from being told no thanks by people you respect.

I did not post about it. I did not message a group chat.

I called Rohan, one of my five. He works in supply chain logistics, a field I still do not fully understand, and he picks up on the second ring, every time. He did not say their loss. He asked me to walk him through the role, what it was, why I wanted it. Then he asked, quietly, whether I actually wanted the work or just wanted them to pick me.

I pushed back. Of course I wanted the work. He did not argue. He just let the question sit there.

By the end of the call I had more or less admitted, at least to myself, that a lot of it had been about being chosen.

You do not get that question from a network. You get it from someone who stuck around long enough to see the real version of you.
