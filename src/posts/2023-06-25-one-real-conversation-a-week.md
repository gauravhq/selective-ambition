---
title: One Real Conversation a Week
date: 2023-06-25
pageDescription: "A weekly reminder to choose one unpredictable conversation over another quick message."
---
Once a week, put one real conversation on the calendar, the same way you would a meeting.

Not a screen. Not a prompt typed into a box that answers back instantly and never gets tired of you. Not a quick text you fire off between two other things. An actual conversation, with a real person whose responses you cannot predict in advance. Call a friend you have been meaning to call. Sit with your partner without a show playing in the background. Talk to someone who might disagree with you, misunderstand you, or say something that surprises you, because that is what real people do and a screen does not.

It is slower than typing a prompt. It is messier too, full of pauses and half-finished sentences and the wrong word chosen and corrected out loud. That is the point, not a flaw to smooth away.

Choose the friction on purpose, once a week, and see what comes back. Not a summary. Not an answer built to please you. Something a little unpredictable, from someone who actually knows you, which is worth more than a faster reply ever could be.
