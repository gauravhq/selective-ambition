---
title: My Actual Scoreboard
date: 2023-10-22
pageDescription: "A short, plain list of what success actually looks like, once the noise is gone."
---
I wrote down what success looks like for me. Not what my industry expects, not what shows up on LinkedIn, just what I actually want. The first attempt, months earlier, was embarrassing. Lines like "build a platform with 100,000 users" and "be known as a thought leader." I read it back and it did not sound like me. It sounded like a bio I was writing for someone else's approval. I threw it out.

The second attempt took an hour. After every line I asked myself the same question. But do you actually want that? Most of the time the answer was no. I would write something down, sit with it, and cross it out.

What I was left with was plain. Make things I am proud of, not necessarily famous or profitable. Have enough money that I do not worry about money, not rich, just not worried. Spend evenings with my family and actually be there. Go deep enough on one or two projects to surprise myself. Sleep well. Keep friends who know the real me.

That is my scoreboard. It is shorter than I expected and a little embarrassing in how simple it is.
