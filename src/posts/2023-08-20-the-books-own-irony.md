---
title: "The Book's Own Irony"
date: 2023-08-20
pageDescription: "Writing about slowing down while still building at full speed is its own contradiction."
---
There is an irony I would rather name myself before you notice it and name it for me. I wrote this book with the same tools that burned me out in the first place. I wrote it during the period I am describing in these pages, not safely after it, once everything had calmed down and I had some distance.

I wrote it because I could not stop building things. Stopping was the theory. Building was still the habit. And a book about stopping felt, at the time, like the one thing worth not stopping for.

There is something almost funny about that, if you squint. A book about selective ambition, about caring for fewer things and letting the rest go, that is itself an act of enormous ambition. Writing it, finishing it, wanting people to read it, all of that is exactly the kind of striving the book keeps asking you to question.

I would rather be honest about that contradiction than pretend it does not exist. I do not think it undoes anything I have written. I think it just means I am still, in some ways, the person I am writing to.
