---
title: Pick Your First 5 Percent
date: 2023-07-09
pageDescription: "How to choose the first small commitment worth quietly dropping from your week."
---
You do not need to redesign your whole life. You need about 5 percent of it back.

Five percent of a week is roughly one evening. Or one project quietly dropped from the "maybe" list. Or one notification turned off for good. Or one commitment you simply stop volunteering for. Small moves, in a life that does not have a lot of empty space to work with.

Here is how to choose your first one. Do not start with the biggest thing wearing you down. That one is loaded with history and hard to touch cleanly. Start with the recurring commitment you resent most, the one you say yes to on autopilot and dread every single time. Drop that one, quietly, without a speech about it.

"My first 5% was a Wednesday evening call I'd been attending out of obligation for six months. Nobody noticed I was gone, which told me everything." That is usually how it goes. The commitment felt load-bearing right up until it was gone, and then it turned out nothing was actually resting on it at all.
