---
title: "Real Constraints Versus the Ones You Added"
date: 2025-02-09
pageDescription: "Half of what feels mandatory is a real constraint, the other half is a habit in disguise."
---
There is a real difference between the constraints that are actually yours and the ones you quietly piled on top without noticing.

Your job is a constraint. Checking email again at ten at night is not. Your boss assigns the work you have to do. Volunteering for the extra project nobody asked you to take on is a completely different thing, even though it can feel identical from the inside, like just one more thing you have no say over.

Most people, when they actually sit down and separate the two piles, find that about half of what is crushing them is genuinely non-negotiable. The job. The bills. The people who depend on them. That half is real, and no amount of better habits will make it lighter.

The other half is habit wearing the costume of obligation. The extra check-in nobody required. The side commitment that started as a casual yes and calcified into something you now describe as "have to." It looks and feels exactly like a real constraint, right up until you ask yourself what would actually happen if you stopped, and realize you are not sure, because you have never once tested it.
