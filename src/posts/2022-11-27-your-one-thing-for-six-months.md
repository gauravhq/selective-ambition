---
title: Your One Thing for Six Months
date: 2022-11-27
pageDescription: "Picking one focus for six months is hard, and the difficulty is the point."
---
Here is the question. If you could only work on one thing for the next six months, just one, what would it be?

Notice what happens the moment you try to answer. For most people it is not a clean, satisfying process. A list starts forming, then a second list of reasons the first list is wrong. Something in you resists narrowing it down at all, as if choosing one thing means quietly admitting the others do not matter as much as you have been pretending.

That resistance is not a malfunction. It is the whole point of the question. If the answer came instantly and easily, you probably would not need to ask it. The friction you feel, the stalling, the urge to say "it depends," or "a few things," is exactly where the useful information lives.

So stay with the discomfort for a minute instead of escaping it. What made this hard? Was it fear of choosing wrong? Loyalty to things you have already started? A quiet suspicion that the honest answer is not the impressive one? The struggle to answer is telling you something the six months themselves never could.
