---
title: Five Hundred Connections, No Real Friends
date: 2024-01-07
pageDescription: "Hundreds of professional connections can still leave you without a single person to call."
---
For about a year I tried to be everywhere professionally. LinkedIn connections, industry events, online communities, coffee chats with people I barely knew. I was building a network, which is what everyone says you are supposed to do, and I was doing it well. Hundreds of connections. An active profile. A calendar full of catch-up calls.

And I had no real friends.

I do not mean that dramatically. I had people I talked to. But I did not have people who knew me, not the professional version I kept polished for meetings, but the actual one. The version that is tired on a Thursday evening and does not feel like being interesting. The version that is worried about something he cannot quite name and does not want advice about it, just company.

Hundreds of connections, and I could not think of five people I would call if something actually went wrong. The network looked full. It was empty in exactly the place that mattered, and I had not noticed the emptiness, because the fullness was so loud and so easy to point to instead.
