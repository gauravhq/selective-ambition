---
title: "What JOMO Actually Means"
date: 2023-02-05
pageDescription: "JOMO is the quiet satisfaction of choosing on purpose to miss out on something."
---
JOMO stands for the Joy of Missing Out. It is the deliberate opposite of FOMO, and the difference between the two is not subtle.

FOMO is anxious. It watches everyone else's plans and wonders if you picked wrong. JOMO is quieter and much less fashionable. It is the specific satisfaction that shows up after you choose, on purpose, to miss something, and then discover that what you kept turned out to be better than what you let go.

That word, choose, matters more than any other part of the definition. JOMO is not laziness wearing a better outfit. It is not giving up, and it is not a polite way of avoiding effort. It is a decision, made with your eyes open, about where your limited time and attention actually belong.

Missing out has always happened to you whether you liked it or not. There was always more you could have done, seen, or joined. JOMO simply asks you to stop treating that fact as a failure and start treating it as a choice, one you get to make on purpose instead of mourning by accident every time.
