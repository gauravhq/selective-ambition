---
title: "Good At It Is Not the Same as Hungry For It"
date: 2025-03-02
pageDescription: "Being talented at a role and actually wanting that role are two completely different things."
---
She was a top performer in sales, the kind of person whose numbers made her the example in other people's training decks. Her managers kept telling her the natural next step was team lead, then regional manager, then director. She nodded along. Eventually she made it her own goal, built a development plan around it, said it out loud in every review.

She got the team lead role. Three months in, she was miserable.

The job was not hard for her. It was simply wrong. She had loved selling, the actual conversations, the specific satisfaction of closing a deal that had been stuck for weeks. Management turned out to be spreadsheets and one-on-ones and mediating disagreements between people who did not want to get along. She was good at it, mostly, because she was good at most things she tried. But good at something and hungry for something are not the same thing, and she had spent years assuming they were.

She stepped back into an individual contributor role and negotiated pay that matched what she actually delivered. Her old managers thought she had lost her nerve. She had never felt more sane.
