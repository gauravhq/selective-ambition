---
title: "The School Play"
date: 2025-01-12
pageDescription: "After missing her son's school play once, a busy HR worker starts dropping committees she never wanted."
---
She worked in HR at a local government office and said yes to every committee that came along, believing visibility was the only path to promotion.

Two years of saying yes. No promotion. She was tired. And then she missed her son's school play for a committee meeting that could have been an email. That was the moment that changed things. She was in a conference room discussing a policy nobody would read when her phone lit up with a photo of her son on stage, in the costume she had helped make.

She did not quit everything at once. She dropped one committee, then another, keeping the one tied to her work and the one she genuinely cared about. For the rest, she sent a short message. "I need to step back from this for now." Nothing more.

Three months later she was passed over for a project lead role and could not tell if the two were connected. That uncertainty sat with her for weeks.

Still, she was in the third row for the next school play, watching her son forget his line and recover, and that mattered more than the meetings she had missed.
