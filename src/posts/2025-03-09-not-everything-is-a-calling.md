---
title: "Not Everything Needs to Be a Calling"
date: 2025-03-09
pageDescription: "Not every project needs to be a calling, some things are worth doing simply because they are fun."
---
It took me a long time to accept this: not everything needs to be your life's calling. Not everything has to be meaningful, or aligned with some larger purpose, to count.

Sometimes you want to build something cool and go home. That is allowed. "Because it is fun," "because I am curious," and "because I want to see if I can" are complete, valid reasons on their own. They do not need a purpose statement stapled to them to be worth doing.

The pressure to find your one true calling is its own kind of FOMO, a nagging sense that a perfect assignment exists out there with your name on it, and you are wasting time until you find it. Most people are not built that way. Most people have a short list of things they enjoy and a short list of things they happen to be good at, and the overlap between those two lists is where the actual work lives.

Not a calling. Just work that does not make you dread Monday morning, which turns out to be a much lower and much more honest bar than the one you were taught to aim for.
