---
title: "When Did You Last Feel Ahead"
date: 2025-11-30
pageDescription: "Ask people when they last felt truly ahead and most go quiet for a while."
---
When was the last time you felt like you were ahead? Not for a moment. Not the brief rush right after the promotion or the shipped project, gone within the hour. I mean really, genuinely, settled in ahead. The kind where you look at your life and your work and think, yes, I am where I need to be, I am not behind.

I asked a few friends this question over the last year, expecting a quick answer. I never got one. What I got instead was a long pause, every time, followed by something like, "I don't think I've ever felt that way." Or, "maybe for a day, after I got the job, but by the next week I was already looking at what's next."

One friend, someone genuinely, visibly successful by any measure, laughed before answering. "I felt ahead once. In 2018. It lasted until I opened LinkedIn."

That line has stayed with me longer than I expected it to. Not because it is unusual. Because it is not. Ask around and you will hear some version of it from almost everyone, including the people whose lives look, from the outside, like the finish line itself.
