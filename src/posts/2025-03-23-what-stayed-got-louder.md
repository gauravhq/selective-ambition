---
title: "What Stayed Got Louder"
date: 2025-03-23
pageDescription: "The wants that survive an honest test get quieter and clearer, not louder or more urgent."
---
Not everything on my list failed the test. A few things made it through, and once they did, they got clearer, not louder in the stressful sense, just easier to see.

There was a writing project, a set of essays about how I think through problems at work. When I asked myself if I would still write them knowing no one would ever read a word, the answer was yes, immediately. There was also a small, boring tool that would save me about twenty minutes every morning and impress nobody at a conference. It excited me in a quieter way, the kind that does not need an audience.

Out of about fifteen things I had been carrying, only four made it through. The other ten fell away, and here is what surprised me. They did not nag. They did not creep back at night asking to be reconsidered. They just left, quietly, like they had been waiting for permission the whole time.

They had felt heavy for months. It turns out they were never actually heavy. I was the one carrying them, and the moment I put them down, so did the weight.
