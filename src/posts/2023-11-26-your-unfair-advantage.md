title: Your Unfair Advantage
date: 2023-11-26
pageDescription: "The problem worth solving is the one your specific life prepared you to see clearly."
---
Andrew Ng has a phrase for this that I think about often. Your unfair advantage.

The idea is simple. The problems worth your time are the ones you can see clearly that other people cannot, because of where you have worked, what you know, who you have been. Not the biggest problem out there. Not the trendiest one. The one your specific, particular life has quietly prepared you to notice, whether you meant it to or not.

When you are picking the one thing to go deep on, the pull is toward the biggest market or the loudest current moment or the most impressive sounding version of your own interests. That pull is worth resisting.

Pick the thing only you can see instead. That is the thing where your years of whatever you have been doing actually become useful, instead of sitting there unused while you chase someone else's game.

Breadth scattered across someone else's domain is just you being mediocre at their game, one generalist among many. Depth in your own domain is you seeing things nobody else in the room sees. That is the entire difference, and it is not a small one.
