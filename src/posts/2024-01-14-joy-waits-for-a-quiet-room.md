---
title: Joy Waits for a Quiet Room
date: 2024-01-14
pageDescription: "Joy waits for a quiet room since it never learned to compete for attention."
---
Joy does not announce itself. It does not post on LinkedIn. It does not send a notification. It does not ping your phone at seven in the morning with a motivational quote.

It cannot out-shout an app built to hold your attention for as long as possible. It was never going to win a loud fight, because the fight was never fair to begin with, and joy does not know how to shout anyway.

So it waits. It waits for a quiet room, a stretch of time with nothing scheduled into it, a moment where nobody is asking you for anything. It shows up in the coffee you actually taste, the dinner you actually cook, the Saturday you actually leave empty. It was never missing. You were just too busy to be in the room when it arrived.

Making the room quiet is not a side effect of choosing to miss out. It is the entire point. You are not clearing your schedule to be more productive later. You are clearing it so joy has somewhere left to land, and time to stay once it does.
