---
title: "Do You Say You're Busy"
date: 2023-01-01
pageDescription: "The word busy can be a complaint, a fact, or a quiet source of pride."
---
Someone asks how you're doing, and the word arrives before you've thought about it. Busy. You say it so often it barely feels like an answer anymore, more like a reflex, a placeholder for whatever is actually happening in your day.

But sit with it for a second. What does that word mean when it comes out of your mouth? Sometimes it's a complaint, said with a sigh, as if the day is a weight you're carrying and want someone to notice. Sometimes it's just a fact, the truest short version of a full calendar. And sometimes, if you're honest, it's a little bit of pride, a quiet way of saying you matter, that your time is in demand.

None of those are wrong. But they are different things, and they lead different places. The next time the question comes and "busy" is already on your tongue, pause before you say it. Ask yourself which one you mean. The answer might tell you more about your week than the word ever did.
