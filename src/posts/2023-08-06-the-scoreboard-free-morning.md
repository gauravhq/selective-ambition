---
title: The Scoreboard-Free Morning
date: 2023-08-06
pageDescription: "A ten-minute morning practice that keeps your phone out of your first cup of coffee."
---
Try this for a week. Before you open any social feed, make your coffee and just sit with it. No scrolling, no comparing, no checking what everyone else did overnight. Ten minutes of a morning that belongs to you, with no scoreboard running underneath it.

The resistance shows up fast. Your thumb will reach for the phone before the coffee is even cool enough to drink. That is not a sign you are doing it wrong. It is the whole point of trying it in the first place: you get to feel the pull and choose something else anyway.

The first morning you try this, you might not make it far. "The first morning I tried this, I lasted four minutes before my thumb went for the phone, but those four minutes were the quietest my brain had been in weeks." That is a real result. Four quiet minutes is not nothing.

Keep going for a week. Not to win. Just to notice what a morning feels like before anyone else gets a vote in it.
