Some commitments you chose on purpose, carefully even, and still do not actually want.

This is the quadrant where intentionality is high and resonance is low. You did not drift into these. You picked them, deliberately, for how they would look rather than how they would feel.

The certification you are pursuing because it will impress the right person. The networking event you show up to because being seen there matters for your reputation. The side project you mention at dinner parties because it makes you sound ambitious, like someone building something worth noticing.

You know the work in each of these. Ask about any of them and you could talk for ten minutes without hesitating. What is missing is not competence. It is love for the thing itself, separate from what it earns you in other people's eyes.

You do not love the work. You love what it signals about you.

That is worth sitting with for a moment, because it is an uncomfortable kind of clarity. Nothing here needs to be abandoned today. It just helps to know, honestly, which of your commitments you chose for the feeling and which you chose for the audience.
