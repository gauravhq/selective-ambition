title: "Written From the Middle"
date: 2026-04-26
pageDescription: "This is written from the middle of the problem, not from some peaceful place beyond it."
---
I am not writing this from the other side of some big transformation, from a calm place where everything is finally peaceful and sorted. I am writing it from the middle, because the middle is where I actually live.

Some days I get it right. I work on one thing. I close the laptop at a reasonable hour. I have dinner without checking my phone once, and it feels like a small, quiet victory. Those days are good days.

Other days I start too many things at once and fall asleep stressed about projects that do not matter to anyone, including me, if I am honest about it. Those days feel familiar in a way I am not proud of.

Last week I caught myself starting a brand new project at eleven at night on a Wednesday, with several unfinished ones already open on my laptop. I knew it was a bad idea while I was doing it. I did it anyway.

So this is not a report from someone who solved the problem. It is a report from someone still living inside it, mostly badly, sometimes well, and trying to be honest about both.
