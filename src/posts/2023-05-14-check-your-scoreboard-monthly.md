---
title: Check Your Scoreboard Monthly
date: 2023-05-14
pageDescription: "A monthly check on whether you are living by your own list or someone else's."
---
Write down three to five things that actually matter to you. Not your boss's KPIs. Not your industry's benchmarks. Not LinkedIn's version of what a good year looks like. Yours. That list is your private scoreboard. Keep it somewhere you will actually see again, not buried in a notes app you never reopen.

Once a month, check in with it. Ask yourself plainly: am I spending my energy on these things, or on someone else's list that snuck back in while I was not paying attention? Be honest about the answer, even when it is uncomfortable. Most months it will be a mix of both, and that is normal.

Adjust as needed. Drop something that stopped mattering. Add something you were afraid to admit you wanted. The list is allowed to change as you do.

The writing part takes ten minutes and feels satisfying, which is exactly why people stop there. The real value is in the recurring check, the monthly return to the same short list, holding your actual life up against it again and again until living by your own scoreboard becomes the default instead of the exception.
