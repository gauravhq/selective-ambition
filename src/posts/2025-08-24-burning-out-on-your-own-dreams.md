---
title: Burning Out on Your Own Dreams
date: 2025-08-24
pageDescription: "The hardest stress to fix is stress that comes from things you love."
---
Here is the trap, and it took me a while to see it clearly. The stress was not coming from things I hated. If it had been, the fix would have been obvious enough. Quit the thing. Delete the thing. Say no to the thing and move on.

But every project keeping me up at night was also a project I genuinely wanted to build. Every source of exhaustion doubled as a source of meaning. I could not remove one without losing the other, because they were the same thing wearing different clothes depending on the hour.

You cannot cut out the source of your stress when the source is also the reason you feel alive in the first place. That is a different problem than being overworked at a job you dislike. There is no clean villain here, no task to hand off, no obviously toxic situation to escape from.

Burning out on your own dreams does not sound like a real problem from the outside looking in. Try explaining it at a dinner party. Try fitting it into a caption under a nice photo. It will not fit anywhere tidy. It is still completely true.
