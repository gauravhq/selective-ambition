---
title: Clearing the Noise to Hear Your Own Voice
date: 2023-08-13
pageDescription: "JOMO is not about doing less, it is about finally hearing your own voice."
---
I do not know if this is permanent. Maybe it is a season. Maybe the fire comes back someday and I start chasing again, and if it does, I hope I am honest enough to let myself notice.

I am trying to hold it lightly. To say "this is working now" instead of "this is the answer forever." That distinction matters more than it sounds like it should. One is a discovery. The other is a promise I am not sure I can keep.

But here is what I do know. The way I was living before was not working, whatever else was true about it. The mornings are quieter now, and quieter turns out to be better, not worse, which surprised me. The people I had almost lost to the screen are still here, and I notice that every single day.

JOMO was never about doing less for the sake of doing less. Less is not the point. It is about clearing the noise for long enough to actually hear your own voice underneath it. And then, once you can hear it, trusting what it says, even when it asks for something smaller than you planned.
