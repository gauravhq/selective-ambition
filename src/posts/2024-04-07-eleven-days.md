---
title: Eleven Days
date: 2024-04-07
pageDescription: "Scrolling through recent calls revealed eleven days since the last real conversation with anyone."
---
It was a Thursday. I had just finished a big piece of work, the kind that makes you want to tell someone right away. Not post about it. Actually tell someone. Share the feeling with a person who would care.

I picked up my phone and scrolled through recent calls, looking for someone to dial. The last real conversation, not a work call, not a quick logistics text, had been eleven days ago. Eleven days. I sat there staring at the screen longer than I want to admit.

I put the phone down. I could not think of a single person to call in that moment, which is its own strange kind of realization to sit with. So I told the AI about the project instead. It answered, "That's a great accomplishment." Which was, technically, the right response. It was also completely empty, in a way I felt immediately.

I sat there afterward in a quiet office, holding a phone full of contacts, feeling like I had no one to call. Not because the contacts were not real people. Because I had let eleven days become the normal distance between us.
