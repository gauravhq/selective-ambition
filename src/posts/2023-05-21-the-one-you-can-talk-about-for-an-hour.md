---
title: The One You Can Talk About for an Hour
date: 2023-05-21
pageDescription: "How to argue yourself down to the one thing worth a whole season."
---
Here is how to actually pick your one thing for this season. Write down everything you think you want to focus on. All of it, no editing yet. Then argue yourself down to one.

"I wrote down seven things and then spent twenty minutes arguing with myself about which six to cut. That argument was the exercise. The six I cut were things I could describe in a sentence. The one I kept was the one I could talk about for an hour." That argument is not a delay before the real work starts. It is the real work.

Here is the tell to watch for while you argue. The things you can sum up in a single sentence and move on from are usually not your one thing, no matter how good they sound on the list. The one that keeps pulling more words out of you, the one you could talk about for a full hour without running out of angles, is the one that is actually alive in you right now.

Everything else goes on the shelf for later. Not deleted. Just waiting its turn while you give this one your full season.
