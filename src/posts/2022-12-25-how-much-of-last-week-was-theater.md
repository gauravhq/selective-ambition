---
title: How Much of Last Week Was Theater
date: 2022-12-25
pageDescription: "Separate what actually got done last week from what only looked like work."
---
Think back over the last week. Actually picture it, day by day, the meetings, the emails, the hours at your desk. Now ask the harder question: how much of that was work that mattered, and how much was performance?

You know the difference even if you don't like to admit it. Actual work moves something forward. It solves a problem, finishes a thing, helps someone. Productivity theater is different. It is being seen working. It is looking busy so nobody asks what you're actually producing. It is performing effort because effort photographs better than results.

Be honest here, because nobody is watching this exercise but you. Go back through the week in your head and sort it into two piles. Not what you meant to do, what you actually did. Some of it will land cleanly in "work." Some of it, if you are truthful, was mostly for show.

This is not about guilt. It is about noticing the gap between the two, because once you can see it, you can start closing it, one hour at a time.
