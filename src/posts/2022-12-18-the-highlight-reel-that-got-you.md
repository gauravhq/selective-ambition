---
title: The Highlight Reel That Got You
date: 2022-12-18
pageDescription: "Naming the exact post that stung takes away some of its power."
---
You know the feeling. You are scrolling, half paying attention, and then one post stops you. A vacation, a promotion, a new house, a body, a relationship that looks effortless. Something in your chest drops a little, even though some part of you knows exactly what a highlight reel is.

That is the strange thing about comparison online. You can understand, intellectually, that nobody posts the argument, the rejection, the boring Tuesday, and still feel the sting anyway. Knowing the trick does not make you immune to it.

So try naming the actual post. Not "social media" in general, the specific one. What was it. Whose life. What exactly made you feel behind. Most people, when pushed to say it out loud, land on something oddly specific and oddly small.

There is something to that specificity. A vague feeling of falling short can follow you around for days, quietly, in the background. A named one, held up and looked at directly, tends to lose some of its grip. It shrinks to the size of one post, on one day, which is closer to the truth.
