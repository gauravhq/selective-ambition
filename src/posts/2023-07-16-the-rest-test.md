---
title: The Rest Test
date: 2023-07-16
pageDescription: "A quick way to tell whether your rest actually recharged you or just numbed you."
---
After whatever you just called rest, run a quick check. Ask yourself: do I feel recharged, or just numb?

The distinction matters more than it sounds like it should. Scrolling for three hours is not rest. Binge-watching a show you do not even like is not rest either, no matter how still your body was for those three hours. And the guilty half-rest, where your brain is still keeping score, is not rest at all. It is just stillness with an argument running underneath it.

Real rest is different. It is choosing to do nothing, on purpose, without guilt attached to it. Not nothing because you collapsed at the end of a long day. Nothing because you decided that doing nothing was allowed, on its own, for this one hour.

If that sounds hard, it is because it is. Most of us have practiced being busy far longer than we have practiced doing nothing on purpose. But this is one of the most important choices in the whole JOMO practice: whether you let rest actually be rest, or you let guilt follow you into the one hour that was supposed to be yours.
