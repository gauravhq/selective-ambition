---
title: Choosing Not To, Out Loud
date: 2023-07-30
pageDescription: "A short sentence to say out loud when guilt has already talked you into something."
---
There is one sentence worth practicing until it becomes automatic: "I could do this. But I'm choosing not to."

Say it when the pull of guilt is stronger than the pull of genuine interest, when your gut says no but your calendar already said yes. Say it out loud, even if no one else is in the room. Not as a performance for anyone watching. Just for yourself, so you hear it as a decision and not a vague feeling you talked yourself out of.

It works even after the fact. Picture this: you already agreed to something. You are sitting in the car on the way there. The wrongness of it finally catches up with you. You go back inside and un-agree. That is allowed. It is awkward, and it is worth it anyway.

The sentence does not need an audience or a clean moment to work. It just needs you to notice the gap between what you agreed to and what you actually want, and to say the true thing before the guilt finishes talking you out of saying it at all.
