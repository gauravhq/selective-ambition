---
title: So Do Less
date: 2025-08-31
pageDescription: "A friend heard a scheduling problem when I was actually describing an identity crisis."
---
I tried to explain it to a friend once, over dinner. I said something like, I think I am doing too much and it is making me miserable.

He took a sip of his beer and said, "So do less."

That was it. Two words, delivered like I had simply overlooked the obvious answer in front of me. I tried again, tried to explain the part where doing less feels like failing at something. He nodded politely. Then he said, "You just need to prioritise better."

I stopped talking about it after that. He was answering a logistics question. I was asking an identity question. I was not saying I have too many tasks on some list. I was saying I do not know who I am if I am not doing all of them, and I could not find the words for that, not at the table, not with a beer in front of me, so I let it drop.

He could not hear what I had never actually said out loud, and I cannot really blame him for that. We talked about football for the rest of the meal instead, which was easier for both of us.
