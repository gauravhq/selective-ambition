---
title: What Do You Do for Fun
date: 2025-08-10
pageDescription: "A simple question at a barbecue revealed how completely work had replaced me."
---
Someone at a barbecue asked what I do, and I answered properly. Work, projects, the tools I was using, for a good two or three minutes straight. I was mid sentence when they said, "Cool. But I meant like, what do you do for fun?"

I had nothing. Genuinely nothing. I stood there holding a paper plate while my brain searched for an answer and came back completely empty. I could not remember the last book I finished that was not about work. Could not remember the last real meal I cooked instead of assembling. Could not remember watching a movie without a phone sitting in my other hand.

I laughed it off. Said something like, "Work is fun," which was not exactly a lie. But the question followed me home anyway. What do you do for fun. The fact that I could not answer it scared me more than any deadline ever had, because it meant something quieter and larger had already happened without my noticing it happen.

Work and self had merged completely. There was no me left standing outside of what I produced, and I had not even seen it happening.
