---
title: What Did Your Last Yes Cost
date: 2022-11-20
pageDescription: "Every yes you give is quietly a no to something else in your life."
---
Think of the last time you said yes when part of you wanted to say no. Not the big dramatic yes, just an ordinary one. A favor, an extra project, a commitment that seemed small enough to agree to without thought.

Now ask what it actually cost you. Not in some abstract sense, specifically. An evening. A weekend. The energy you would have spent on something else, something that mattered more to you but never got the chance to compete for your time. Yeses rarely announce their price upfront. You usually find out afterward, when you are tired and cannot say why.

That is the part worth sitting with. Every yes you give is also a no to something else, whether you name it that way or not. Saying yes to the extra project was a quiet no to the evening you meant to keep for yourself. Saying yes to someone else's request was a no to your own plan, even if you never said the word out loud.

So go back to that last yes. What did it push out of your life to make room for itself? And knowing that now, would you still say it?
