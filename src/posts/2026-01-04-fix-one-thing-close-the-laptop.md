---
title: "Fix One Thing, Then Close the Laptop"
date: 2026-01-04
pageDescription: "Fixing one thing today and closing the laptop protects the evening you will not get back."
---
Here is a shift I am still trying to make, one evening at a time. Decide what you are going to fix today. Just that one thing. Fix it. Then close the laptop.

The other bugs will still be there tomorrow. That part is guaranteed. They are not going anywhere, because software breaks, whether a person wrote it or a machine did, and there will always be another list waiting behind this one. You will never clear it. Not this week, not this year. That is simply what the list is like.

Your evening is a different story. It is not waiting for you the way the bugs are. It is going somewhere, and once it is gone you do not get it back. Not the specific hour, and not the version of you that existed inside it, slightly less tired than the version that shows up tomorrow.

So protect it on purpose. Fix the one thing you decided to fix. Resist the pull of the second thing, and the third, even though both are right there, tempting, apparently quick. Close the laptop anyway. The list will forgive you. It always does, mostly because it never actually ends.
