---
title: Burning the Garlic
date: 2024-02-25
pageDescription: "Cooking dinner by hand, burnt garlic and all, turned out to be its own quiet reward."
---
I cooked dinner one evening last month. Not ordered, not reheated, actually cooked. I chopped onions and burned the garlic, because I always burn the garlic. Twenty years of cooking and I still cannot judge the heat right. I scraped the pan and started again.

The dal took about forty minutes. Cumin seeds crackled in the oil, onions went translucent, turmeric turned everything yellow, and at some point the smell in the kitchen stopped being a cooking smell and became something specific. My mother's kitchen, though I could not have told you that while it was happening.

I did not check my phone while it simmered. Not discipline, I just forgot. I was stirring, adding salt, tasting, adding a little more.

I chose to miss out on being productive during dinner that night. Nobody gave me an award for it. No post online, no reel about it the next morning. Just dal and rice, and the small, stupid satisfaction of making something with my hands that was not a product, was not a project, and would not go on anyone's portfolio.
