---
title: Busy Versus Stretched
date: 2025-06-22
pageDescription: "Being busy and being stretched thin are different problems with different fixes."
---
There is a real difference between being busy and being stretched, and mixing the two up costs you more than you would expect.

Busy is a schedule problem. Too much to do, not enough hours to do it in. That is annoying, but it is solvable. You can reorganise. You can delegate something. You can drop a task that turns out not to matter as much as you first thought.

Stretched is different, and no amount of reorganising touches it. Stretched has nothing to do with hours. You are short on yourself. You have spread your attention, your care, your actual presence across so many things that each one only gets a thin, watered down slice of who you are. Nothing gets your full attention, because nothing is left holding more than a fraction of it.

You are not running out of hours in the day. You are running out of yourself, which is a far harder thing to notice and a far harder thing to fix with a schedule change.

No calendar app was built for that problem. Calendars manage time. They have nothing at all to say about a self that has been divided one time too many.
