---
title: The Crisp Apple
date: 2023-11-19
pageDescription: "Starting something new feels bright and quick, but staying with it feels warmer and deeper."
---
There are two different flavors of satisfaction, and I did not have words for the difference until I had felt both enough times to compare them.

The satisfaction of starting something new is bright and thin, like biting into a crisp apple. It is good. It is real. But it is quick, gone almost as soon as you notice it, and it asks you to go find another apple.

The satisfaction of staying with something past the point where it is easy, into the stretch that is just hard work and slow progress and the occasional small victory, is a different thing entirely. It is quieter. Warmer. It builds instead of spiking. It does not announce itself the way new things do, so you can go a long time without noticing it is even there.

Then one day you look up and realize you have built something that is actually yours, not a sketch, not an idea you mentioned to people, but a real thing that came from showing up.

Depth does not feel like efficiency. It feels like discovery, like finding something that was there all along and only became visible because you stayed.
