---
title: The Uncle Who Started Painting at 57
date: 2023-09-17
pageDescription: "An uncle who retired into painting shows that everyone's pace can be different."
---
Some people build empires at 25. Good for them, genuinely. If you are 25 and what you built makes you happy, your pace works.

Some people find their thing at 50, or later, and that is fine too. My uncle started painting at 57. He had been an accountant his whole working life, a quiet numbers guy nobody would have guessed had any interest in art. He retired, did nothing for three months, and then walked into a community art class mostly because his wife was tired of him sitting on the couch.

The first paintings were terrible, by his own account. The third one was bad in a different way, which he said felt like progress. Small steps, but real ones.

Now he paints small watercolours every morning and wakes up looking forward to the day, something he could not say for his last fifteen years of accounting. His brother, my father, still asks him every so often when he is going to do something useful with his time. But his pace, slow, unrushed, no deadline, no audience, is exactly right for him. Nobody else gets a vote on that.
