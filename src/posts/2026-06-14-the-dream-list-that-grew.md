---
title: "The Dream List That Only Grew"
date: 2026-06-14
pageDescription: "A list of ideas grew for years while nothing on it ever became real."
---
I kept a dream list. Not a to-do list, a dream list, the kind you add to in the shower or at eleven at night when the ideas will not stop coming. Mine lived in a Google Doc, and the first entry is dated somewhere around 2019, though some of the ideas were older than that.

Apps I wanted to build. Websites I wanted to launch. Products I could picture so clearly in my head that they felt like they already existed somewhere, just out of reach.

I would open the doc maybe once a month. Scroll through it. Add a new idea or two. Feel a small thrill at how good it looked on paper. Then close it and go back to whatever I was actually supposed to be doing that day.

The doc grew steadily for years. Nothing else did.

That gap is the part worth sitting with. Imagining something and building it are not two ends of the same activity. They barely use the same muscles. A list can grow forever without a single idea on it ever becoming real, and mine very nearly did.
