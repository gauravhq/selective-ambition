---
title: "The Ten-Item List"
date: 2024-10-06
pageDescription: "Ten real project ideas on one list made it obvious that all ten could not survive."
---
I once sat down and wrote out my actual project list, not a to-do list, the big one, and it had ten items on it.

There was the analytics dashboard, a tool for tracking personal metrics that would pull data from five different places. I had sketched wireframes on the back of an envelope almost two years earlier and never gone much further.

There was the eight-module writing course. I had an outline and a practice intro I recorded once and hated the sound of my own voice too much to ever open the file again.

There was the mentorship matching app. I had a schema sketched out and a domain name bought, which felt like progress at the time, though really it was just spending twelve dollars to feel productive.

And there were seven more, each with a name, a folder, and its own small claim on my future.

Looking at the full list at once, I felt the usual mix of excitement and guilt. But underneath it was a plainer fact I had been avoiding. I could not do all ten. Not this year, probably not in five years, and definitely not well.
