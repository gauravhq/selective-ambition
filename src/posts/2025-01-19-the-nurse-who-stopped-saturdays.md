---
title: "The Nurse Who Stopped the Saturday Shift"
date: 2025-01-19
pageDescription: "A nurse quietly stops an extra Saturday shift and rediscovers what a weekend feels like."
---
For two years she picked up the extra Saturday shift at the hospital. Not really for the money. Because everyone else on her floor did it, and staying home while her colleagues worked felt like letting the team down.

Then one month, she stops. She still works her full weekday schedule. She just stops volunteering for the extra day.

The first Saturday she stays home, she wakes at five thirty anyway, body on autopilot, and lies there wondering who is covering and whether someone will text. She makes coffee and sits at her kitchen table in a silence so unfamiliar it almost feels loud. Three years in that apartment, and she never noticed her kitchen gets morning sun.

The guilt hums until about noon. Then she lies down, falls asleep in minutes, and wakes up an hour later feeling normal, not exhausted, not wired, just a person with nowhere to be. That evening she cooks a real meal and eats it at the table instead of standing at the counter.

Nobody fires her. Nobody even mentions it. Her colleagues are a little cooler with her for a while, a small, real cost for a Saturday that was actually hers.
