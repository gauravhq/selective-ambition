---
title: "The Canyon Between an Idea and Building It"
date: 2026-06-07
pageDescription: "Wanting to build something and knowing how to build it turned out to be two very different things."
---
For years, the list stayed a list.

I tried to learn to code more than once. Python, then JavaScript. I got through the early lessons every time, variables, loops, the basic stuff, and then stalled right around the point where you are supposed to start building something real. The gap between what I could imagine and what I could actually make exist was not a gap. It was a canyon.

Hiring someone was the other option, and I looked into that too. I got a quote once, for a fairly simple tool, and the number made me close the laptop and just sit there for a minute. I could not even tell if the price was fair, because I did not know enough about the work to judge it.

So instead of building, I made small private acts of hope. One-pagers nobody read. Wireframes sketched in a notebook. A pitch deck I was proud of that I saved to a folder and never opened again. A domain I bought late one night that sat there pointing at nothing until I let it expire.

Not nothing. But not building, either.
