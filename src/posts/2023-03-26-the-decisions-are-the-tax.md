---
title: "The Decisions Are the Tax"
date: 2023-03-26
pageDescription: "Just thinking about too many possible projects quietly drains your energy before you act on any."
---
You do not have to finish thirty projects to feel exhausted by them. Just thinking about all thirty can be enough.

Roy Baumeister's ego-depletion model proposed that every decision, no matter how small, draws from the same limited pool of mental energy. Deciding what to eat for breakfast uses some of the same fuel as deciding whether to start a new project. The model has faced real replication challenges in recent years. But the lived experience holds up even if the mechanism is debated. Having thirty possible projects sitting in your head costs you energy, whether or not you act on a single one of them.

That is the part people miss. It is not the work itself that drains you first. It is the deciding, the low hum of which project to pick up next. The decisions are the tax. The actions that follow are almost a relief by comparison, because at least they resolve something.

I noticed this most sharply on days with too many AI tools open at once, tabs everywhere, each one asking quietly for a choice. I had not produced anything by mid-afternoon, but I was already tired, spent entirely on deciding.
