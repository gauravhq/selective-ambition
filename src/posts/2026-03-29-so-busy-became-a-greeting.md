---
title: "When So Busy Became a Greeting"
date: 2026-03-29
pageDescription: "Saying I'm so busy stopped being a complaint and turned into a way of bragging."
---
At some point, saying "I'm so busy" stopped being a complaint. It turned into a greeting. A flex. A quick way of saying "I'm important, I'm needed, I'm in demand," all packed into a few casual words at the start of a conversation.

The opposite became something nobody wanted to admit to. Not busy started to sound like not valuable. Not trying hard enough. Falling behind while everyone else keeps moving.

I do not know exactly when the shift happened. I do not think there was a single moment you could point to and say, there, that is when it changed. Maybe it was always a little bit true, some quiet current running under everything, and the internet just turned the volume up until it was impossible to ignore.

What I do know is how normal it feels now to answer how are you with so busy, and how strange it would feel to answer with genuinely fine, not much going on. One answer sounds like a life in motion. The other sounds, to most ears, like an apology waiting to happen. That gap is worth noticing.
