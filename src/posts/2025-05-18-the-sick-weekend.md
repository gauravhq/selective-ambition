---
title: The Sick Weekend
date: 2025-05-18
pageDescription: "Getting properly sick for one weekend showed me what real rest feels like."
---
I got properly sick once. Fever, aches, the kind where sitting up makes the whole room tilt sideways. I spent two full days in bed doing absolutely nothing. No laptop within reach. No projects waiting on the other side of a screen somewhere.

By Sunday evening the fever had broken, and I noticed a feeling I had not had in months. Empty, but the clean kind of empty. Like a room after you have finally moved all the furniture out and can see, for the first time in ages, how big the space actually is underneath everything you had crammed into it for years.

For two days I had not been the person with a dozen projects running at once. I had just been a sick guy in a bed, doing nothing anyone could measure or credit, and it turned out to be restful in a way that went deeper than sleep alone.

Monday morning, back at work, the old feeling returned within the hour, right on schedule as always. But I remembered Sunday evening clearly enough to think one thing. You should not have to get sick just to feel that.
