---
title: "Write It Down and See the Seams"
date: 2025-02-02
pageDescription: "Writing your commitments down on paper reveals which ones are obligations and which are just habits."
---
The list you carry in your head feels like it is all mandatory. Every item feels urgent. Every commitment feels impossible to drop without something breaking.

Write it down instead. On paper, with an actual pen, not a note on your phone. Something shifts when you can see the whole list at once instead of feeling it as one heavy, undifferentiated weight. You start noticing the seams, the places where real obligation ends and plain habit begins, where "I have to do this" is quietly just "I have always done it this way" wearing a more serious costume.

One man made this list and counted seventeen ongoing commitments sitting outside his actual job. When asked to mark which ones would have a real, tangible consequence if he simply stopped, he marked six.

Six out of seventeen. The other eleven were not a constraint problem at all. They were a permission problem. He had never actually asked himself whether he was allowed to stop, so he had not stopped, and the list had grown quietly for years, one casual yes at a time, until it looked as fixed and as real as rent.
