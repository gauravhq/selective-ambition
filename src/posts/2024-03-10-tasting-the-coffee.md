---
title: Tasting the Coffee
date: 2024-03-10
pageDescription: "Fifteen years of drinking coffee, and it took leaving the phone upstairs to actually taste it."
---
It's early, maybe six, maybe earlier, and the house is still dark. The coffee machine is an old drip model with a glass carafe, and it clears its throat as the water pushes through, a small hiss of steam, then the steady drip into the pot. That sound used to annoy me. Now it feels like a day beginning on my own terms.

I hold the blue mug, the one with the chip on the handle I keep meaning to throw away. It fits my hand too well to let go of. Outside the window the street is that grey-blue color it gets before the sun shows up, everything looking like a pencil drawing, a cat sitting completely still on the wall across the road.

My phone is upstairs. I didn't leave it there on purpose. I just forgot it existed for a few minutes.

The coffee is good, not remarkable, and I am actually tasting it now. The warmth first, then a round bitterness, a faint sweetness underneath. Fifteen years of drinking coffee, and I only started tasting it about three months ago.
