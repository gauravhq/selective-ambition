---
title: "The Nobody-Knew Test"
date: 2025-04-06
pageDescription: "One quiet question tells you whether a want is truly yours or borrowed from someone else."
---
Here is a test for sorting real wants from borrowed ones. Take anything on your list and ask: would I still want this if nobody ever knew I did it?

Say you want to learn guitar. Picture the version where you get good but never play for another human being. No audience, no video, no one finding out. If the answer is still yes, that is real hunger, plain and simple. If the honest answer is closer to "actually, I mostly want people to know I can play," that is a borrowed desire. Not a bad one. Just not yours.

The test has a blind spot worth naming. Some good work is social by design, teaching, mentoring, building something with other people, and you cannot do those in a locked room. For anything like that, try a different version of the question: would I still do this even if nobody ever thanked me for it?

Either way, the point is the same. Borrowed wants and real ones can look identical from the outside. They carry the same urgency, the same excitement. The only place they show their true colors is in secret.
