---
title: "Productivity Theater"
date: 2026-03-22
pageDescription: "There is a real difference between looking like you are working and doing work that matters."
---
There is a term for the gap between looking like you are working and actually doing work that matters. Productivity theater. Once you learn the phrase, you start seeing it everywhere, in your own habits and in everyone else's.

It is performance art for the corporate world. You are not doing more work. You are making the work you already do more visible, which is a completely different skill and, for a lot of people, an easier one.

The uncomfortable part is that it works. The people who perform busyness well often do get promoted. They do get noticed in meetings. They do earn a reputation for being dedicated, even when the label has more to do with timing than output. And everyone watching draws the obvious conclusion. If that is what gets rewarded, that is what I need to do too.

So the cycle keeps going, one visible email and one packed calendar at a time, not because anyone sat down and designed it this way, but because it keeps getting quietly rewarded, and rewarded behavior repeats. That is the whole mechanism, once you see it.
