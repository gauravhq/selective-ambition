---
title: What Are You Afraid of Missing
date: 2022-11-06
pageDescription: "The fear of missing out usually shrinks once you actually sit inside it."
---
Picture one full day this weekend with no news, no social media, no feeds of any kind. Not a detox retreat, just one ordinary day. Before you even try it, notice what your mind does with the idea. For most people, some version of fear shows up almost immediately.

What is it, specifically, that you are afraid of missing? Say it plainly instead of leaving it vague. Are you worried about a message you would miss, a piece of news you would hear about late, a sense of being out of the loop among people whose opinions matter to you? Or is it something less specific, a general dread of falling behind?

That is usually where the fear does not hold up. The messages will still be there Monday. The news will still be there, mostly unchanged. The feeling of missing out is loudest in imagination, before the day has even started. It tends to shrink considerably once you are actually inside the day instead of just dreading it from outside.

So name your fear before the weekend arrives. Then test it against one real day, and see which one was telling the truth.
