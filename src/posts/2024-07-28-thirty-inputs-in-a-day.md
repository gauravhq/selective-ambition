---
title: Thirty Inputs in a Day
date: 2024-07-28
pageDescription: "A single ordinary Tuesday held thirty small inputs, and each one quietly took a piece of attention."
---
I timed a normal Tuesday once, just to see what was actually happening.

Before my feet touched the floor, three notifications had already arrived. Then Instagram, just to check, which is never actually just a check. Email while I made breakfast. A commute podcast that named three tools I had never heard of, each one landing like a small piece of homework I never agreed to take on.

At work it kept going. Slack, then Jira, then Notion, then a dashboard I did not really need to look at yet. The afternoon repeated the morning almost exactly. By evening I had Netflix on and Reddit open at the same time, half doing both, fully doing neither.

When I counted everything that had entered my head that day, I stopped at thirty. Thirty separate inputs, and none of them felt big enough to matter on its own.

But each one took something. Not much. Just a little piece of attention, a little piece of energy, before I had even chosen what I wanted my day to be about.
