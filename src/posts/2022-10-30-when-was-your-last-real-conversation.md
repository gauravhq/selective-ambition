---
title: When Was Your Last Real Conversation
date: 2022-10-30
pageDescription: "Try to name the last time you had a real, undistracted conversation."
---
Think about the last time you had a real conversation. Not a check-in, not a logistics exchange about who is picking up what or what time something starts. An actual conversation, the kind where you were not glancing at your phone, not half-composing your next sentence while the other person was still talking, not performing listening while actually waiting for your turn.

When was that. Try to name the day, or at least the rough week. Some people can answer instantly, a specific evening with a specific person still glowing in their memory. Others have to think for an uncomfortably long time, scrolling back through weeks that were full of talking and strangely empty of anything that felt like real conversation.

If it takes you a while to land on an answer, that is worth noticing without immediately feeling bad about it. It is simply information about how your days have been spent, and who has actually had your full attention lately, instead of a version of you that was present in the room but somewhere else in your head.

Who would you want your next real conversation to be with?
