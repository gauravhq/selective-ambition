---
title: Real Rest Versus Numbing
date: 2024-02-11
pageDescription: "Scrolling on the couch is not rest, and neither is a mind still quietly keeping score."
---
What replaced the guilt was a word I had been missing. Rest.

Not the kind where you collapse on the couch and scroll for three hours and end up feeling worse than when you sat down. That is not rest. That is numbing.

Not the kind where you are technically off the clock but your brain is quietly running a background calculation of everything you should be doing instead. That is not rest either. That is guilt wearing a bathrobe, pretending to relax.

Real rest is the kind where your brain actually stops keeping score. Where there is no open tab in your mind holding a to-do list. Where you are genuinely, completely, almost uselessly unproductive, and it does not bother you at all.

It feels like putting down a bag you forgot you were carrying. You did not know it was heavy until it was not there anymore, and your shoulders remember before your mind does.

I had spent years confusing being off with being at rest. They are not the same thing, and mixing them up is how you end up exhausted on a day you did nothing.
