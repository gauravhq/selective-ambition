---
title: The Floor Two Degrees Off Level
date: 2023-12-10
pageDescription: "A small flaw made early only becomes visible to someone who stays long enough to see it."
---
Here is what I saw at month three that I never would have seen in week one. The project had a flaw in its foundation. Not a bug. A design choice I had made early on, back when everything was exciting and I was moving fast, that seemed harmless at the time but was quietly making everything harder.

It was like building a house on a floor that is two degrees off level. You do not notice at first. But by month three, every new thing you add leans a little. Doors do not close right. Furniture slides toward the wall on its own.

I only saw it because I had been inside the project long enough for the pattern to become visible. If I had been bouncing between five projects, I would never have stayed long enough to feel the lean. I would have blamed the tools, or my own ability, or bad luck.

Instead I spent a weekend rebuilding the foundation. Tedious, unglamorous work. Nobody would have cheered for it, and nobody did. But everything that came after was easier, and the whole project finally breathed the way it was supposed to.
