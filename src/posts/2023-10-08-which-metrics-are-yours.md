title: Knowing Which Metrics Are Yours
date: 2023-10-08
pageDescription: "The skill is knowing which metrics are truly yours and which ones you only borrowed."
---
The hard part is not writing the list. The hard part is living by it, day after day, when the rest of the world keeps a different score.

You still have a boss, and KPIs do not care about your private scoreboard. Your industry runs on benchmarks, not on what quietly matters to you. Your family has expectations, spoken and unspoken, and kids who need things that cost real money. That money comes from the public game, not the private one.

So the skill is not ignoring the public scoreboard. That is not realistic. The actual skill is knowing which metrics are yours and which ones you are only borrowing.

Do enough in the public game to stay in it. Meet the targets, show up, hit the number that gets asked of you. Then save your real energy for the scoreboard nobody assigned you, the one you actually have to answer to.
