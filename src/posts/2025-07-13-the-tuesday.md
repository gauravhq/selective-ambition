---
title: The Tuesday
date: 2025-07-13
pageDescription: "The realization that arrives only once you stop running long enough to check direction."
---
It was a Tuesday. Unremarkable, which might be exactly why it happened on one. Nothing important ever happens on a Tuesday, so nobody stays on guard.

I was at my desk, just after nine. Coffee sitting to my left, cold, untouched since eight thirty, a ring inside the mug from all the other coffees I had made that month and forgotten to drink. The fluorescent light above me had a small flicker, nothing dramatic, just a pulse every few seconds that sat at the edge of my attention like something I could not quite place.

Three sticky notes were stuck to the monitor, each carrying a single word or abbreviation I no longer understood. One was in my own handwriting and meant absolutely nothing to me anymore.

And somewhere in that ordinary, slightly broken morning, a question arrived that I had apparently been avoiding for months. Not dramatically. Just quietly, the way a thought waits until the room is finally empty enough to be heard clearly.

That is the Tuesday. Not one dramatic moment, just the first time you stop moving long enough to ask whether you are running in the right direction at all.
