---
title: "Don't Run Projects in Parallel Just Because You Can"
date: 2024-08-11
pageDescription: "Running three projects at once for six weeks moved all three nowhere at all."
---
The tools now make it possible to run three projects at once. The app in the morning, the website in the afternoon, the side project after dinner. The AI doing the actual typing does not get tired, so it is tempting to ask why you should behave as if you do.

Because you are not the AI. You are the person supervising it, and your brain does not context-switch for free. Every jump from one project to another leaves a little piece of your attention behind on the thing you just left.

I tried it anyway. I ran three projects in parallel for about six weeks. At the end of those six weeks, all three were roughly where they had started. I had put in real hours on each of them, but the work was thin, the kind you produce when part of your mind is still stuck on whatever you were doing an hour earlier.

I dropped two and kept one. In the two weeks that followed, that single project moved further than all three combined had moved in the previous six. Speed was never the tools' problem to solve. It was mine.
