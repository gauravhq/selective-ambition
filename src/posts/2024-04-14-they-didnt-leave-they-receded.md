---
title: "They Didn't Leave, They Receded"
date: 2024-04-14
pageDescription: "People rarely leave dramatically but quietly stop expecting you to show up at all."
---
Over the course of a year, the people in my life quietly stopped reaching for me. Not dramatically. Nobody sat me down and left. There was no confrontation, no final argument, no clean break I could point to later and say that is when it happened.

My wife stopped asking about my projects, because the answer was always some distracted summary I gave without really looking up from the screen. A friend started texting less, because my replies had started taking days instead of minutes, and eventually they stopped expecting a quick one back.

They did not leave. That is the part I want to be clear about, because leaving would have been easier to notice. They adjusted instead. They recalibrated what to expect from me, quietly and without complaint, the way people do when they have asked for your attention enough times and learned the answer.

They just stopped expecting me to be there. And I was too busy building things, important sounding things, to notice that the people around me had rewritten the terms of the relationship without telling me.
