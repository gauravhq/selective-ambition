---
title: Expectation Inflation
date: 2025-05-25
pageDescription: "New tools did not create free time, they just raised what everyone expects of you."
---
The old version of the myth said, "You can have it all if you work hard enough." The new version says, "You can build anything now, so why aren't you building everything?"

I felt this shift before I had a name for it. At a quarterly review, a slide showed a genuinely impressive number. Tasks that used to take two full days could now be finished in about four hours. Good news, on its face. Then the next slide appeared, titled "Revised Output Targets."

The time that got saved was never mine to keep. It had already been spoken for, filled in before I even noticed it existed as free time at all. The new expectation was five things done in the time it used to take to finish two, and nobody in that room questioned it out loud. I certainly did not either.

Enough stopped being a place you could actually arrive at and stay. The definition kept moving every quarter, redrawn by someone with a slide deck and a straight face. The tools did not free anyone up. They just quietly raised the bar and called the result progress.
