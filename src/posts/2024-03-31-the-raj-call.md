---
title: The Raj Call
date: 2024-03-31
pageDescription: "A call with no agenda turned brutal small talk into the conversation someone actually needed."
---
I called my friend Raj with no reason and no agenda, just to try the once a week real conversation I had been telling myself I would start.

The first five minutes were brutal. "How's it going." "Good, good." Then silence, the kind where you can hear the other person breathing and your brain starts screaming that this whole call is inefficient, that you could be doing something useful right now instead.

Then Raj mentioned that his dad was sick. Not dying, but sick enough that he had been driving to the hospital every other day for three weeks. He had not told anyone. Not because it was a secret. Simply because no one had picked up the phone to ask.

I listened for forty minutes. No advice, no solutions, nothing clever to offer. Just listening while a friend said something true out loud for what sounded like the first time.

When we hung up he said, "Thanks for calling, man. I needed that." And I realized I needed it too, in a different way.

That is what friction gives you. The chance to actually matter to someone, in a way that costs you something real.
