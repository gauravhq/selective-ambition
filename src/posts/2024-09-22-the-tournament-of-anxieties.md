---
title: "The Tournament of Anxieties"
date: 2024-09-22
pageDescription: "After choosing one project, the fear that showed up was oddly specific and lasted about a week."
---
After I picked the one project and shelved the other nine, the fear that showed up was more specific than I expected.

I kept picturing a particular nightmare. Someone announcing the exact tool I had just set aside, except built better, shipped faster, and already getting traction.

There was a quieter fear underneath that one, and it bothered me more. What if the project I picked is actually the worst one on the list? What if I have terrible judgment and just spent real time proving it?

At night I would replay the choice, running every idea against every other idea in my head like a tournament bracket, seeding and reseeding them, organizing a whole sports league out of my own anxieties, as if enough reshuffling would produce a safer answer.

For about a week, that fear was the main thing I felt. No relief for having finally chosen. No clarity about whether I had chosen well. Just nine other possible futures arguing with the one I had actually picked.
