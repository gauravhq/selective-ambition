---
title: "Where Your Ambitions Come From"
date: 2025-04-20
pageDescription: "Your ambitions arrive from feeds, industries, and peers long before you ever choose them yourself."
---
Trace it. Actually trace where your ambitions came from, instead of assuming they are yours by default.

LinkedIn shows you what success looks like: the promotions, the career moves, the humble-brag posts that read like humility but function like a highlight reel. Instagram shows you what experiences to want, the trips, the meals, the mornings that look nothing like yours. Your industry tells you what skills to have. This year it is AI. Last year it was data. Before that, agile. The must-have skill turns over every eighteen months or so, which means you are always exactly one certification behind and never quite caught up.

Then there are your peers. Someone bought a house at thirty. Someone else made partner, started a company, had a kid, and each of those becomes a data point your brain quietly files under where you should be by now, even though you never agreed to that particular race.

None of this happens on purpose. Nobody sits in a room deciding what you should want. It happens through exposure, through repetition, layer after layer, until your want list belongs to everyone but you.
