---
title: "Guilt Means You're Carrying Too Many Contracts"
date: 2023-04-16
pageDescription: "Guilt is often just a sign that you are carrying too many unspoken contracts at once."
---
Guilt has a job. It is not random static, it is your brain signaling that you have broken a social contract, real or imagined. That system worked fine when you had a handful of contracts to track. The trouble now is how many you are carrying at once.

You feel guilty for not working late, because of an unspoken contract with your employer. Guilty for not pursuing the side project, a contract with your own ambition. Guilty for resting, a contract with a culture that treats busyness as proof of worth. Guilty for even complaining about having too many opportunities, a contract with everyone who has fewer.

None of these signals is loud on its own. Stacked together, they create a low hum that makes rest feel wrong and stopping feel dangerous.

Here is the useful part. The guilt is not proof that you are doing something wrong. It is proof that you are carrying too many contracts at once. I counted mine once and found more running than I had hours in the week to honor. Naming them did not erase the guilt, but it turned a vague heaviness into something I could actually renegotiate.
