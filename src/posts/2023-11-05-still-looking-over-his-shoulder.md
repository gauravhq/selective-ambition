---
title: Still Looking Over His Shoulder
date: 2023-11-05
pageDescription: "Choosing depth never fully ends the doubt, only teaches you to keep walking while carrying it."
---
I should be honest about this part. I still open the shelved ideas list sometimes, usually late at night, when the current project is stuck or boring or just hard in the way depth is hard.

I scroll through the ones I said no to and feel the pull. What if that one was the better bet. What if I chose wrong. What if I am three years into the wrong thing and the right one was sitting in that list the whole time, waiting.

The choice to go deep is not clean. It is messy in a way nobody tells you about in advance. Some nights I think I am just telling myself a tidy story about focus and depth to justify being too scared to try all of them and find out, honestly, which ones I would have failed at.

That is the true version. Not someone who figured it out and now knows better than you. Someone who picked a direction, is still walking in it, and still looks over his shoulder more often than he would like to admit, even now, even after all this time.
