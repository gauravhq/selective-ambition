---
title: "What Borrowed Desires Are"
date: 2023-01-22
pageDescription: "Borrowed desires are wants you absorbed from other people rather than chose for yourself."
---
Borrowed desires are wants that did not actually start with you. They arrived from somewhere else first, LinkedIn, Instagram, your industry, your peers, your parents, or the wider culture, and then quietly settled in as if they had always been yours.

They do not feel borrowed. They feel urgent, specific, and completely real, which is exactly what makes them hard to spot from the inside. The way to check is the nobody knew test. Ask yourself a plain question about each one. Would you still want this if nobody else ever found out you had it? If the honest answer is no, the desire was likely borrowed. If the answer is yes, it might actually be yours.

In my own experience, applying that test to my to-do list was not a comfortable exercise. About 80 percent of what was on it turned out to be borrowed, absorbed from somewhere outside myself and never actually chosen.

That number is not a verdict on you. It is just a starting point, a way to see which items on your list deserve your actual energy and which ones were only ever noise wearing a familiar voice.
