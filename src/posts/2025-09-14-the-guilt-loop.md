---
title: The Guilt Loop
date: 2025-09-14
pageDescription: "Doing more to quiet the fear of falling behind only feeds the fear further."
---
Here is the loop, laid out plainly.

You feel like you are not doing enough. A project you have not started. A skill you keep meaning to learn. The feeling is vague but it does not go away, a low hum sitting behind everything else you do all day.

So you do more. You start the project. You add another thing to the list. For a moment the hum quiets down. You are doing something. You are back in the game, however briefly.

Then the hum returns, because now you are doing more, but doing it badly. You are stretched thin. You are snapping at people who did nothing to deserve it. You are starting things and not finishing them. And a second feeling arrives, right on top of the first one: you are doing too much.

Not doing enough and doing too much, at the same time, about the very same life. That makes no logical sense. Stress does not care about logic. It only cares about the gap between where you think you should be and where you actually are.

The gap never closes. It cannot, because the "should" keeps expanding faster than you can chase it.
