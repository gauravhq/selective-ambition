---
title: Five People
date: 2023-12-31
pageDescription: "Knowing five people deeply beats knowing five hundred people well enough to forget their names."
---
I let the networking go. Stopped the events, stopped the coffee chats with strangers, stopped accepting every connection like it was some professional obligation I owed the internet. I put that time, six or seven hours a week I had not realized I was giving away, into five people instead.

Real friends. The kind you call when something goes wrong.

When you know five people deeply, you remember things. Their kid's school troubles. The career change they have not told their manager about yet. The small worry they mentioned once in passing that you did not forget. When you know five hundred people shallowly, you do not remember anything. You just have a contacts list, and a contacts list cannot call you back when you need it to.

I started knowing the details of their lives between calls, not out of effort but because I finally had room in my head for them. That room had been full of people I barely knew. Once it emptied out, there was space for five people to actually move in and stay.

Fewer people, held closer. That was the whole trade.
