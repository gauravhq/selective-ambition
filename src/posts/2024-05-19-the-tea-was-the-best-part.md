---
title: The Tea Was the Best Part of My Day
date: 2024-05-19
pageDescription: "A slow, distracted kitchen conversation about nothing turned out to matter more than the work."
---
I closed the laptop at eight, with at least two more productive hours left in me. Mid flow, which is the most dangerous time to stop, because momentum starts to feel sacred and stopping feels like betrayal.

I went to the kitchen anyway. Warm yellow light from the bulb we keep meaning to replace. The smell of cardamom. My wife pouring hot water into two cups, and she had already made one for me before I even walked in.

The conversation that followed was slow and distracted and full of pauses. Groceries. Her sister. A podcast she had heard about old Bollywood playback singers. Nobody said anything especially interesting. Nobody had a breakthrough. If you had recorded it and played it back, you would have found nothing worth quoting.

Lying in bed that night, running through the day, that kitchen conversation was the thing I came back to. Not the project. Not the code that finally worked after days of not working.

It was the best part of my day, and I almost missed it for two more hours at a desk.
