---
title: "I'm Not Doing That"
date: 2023-09-10
pageDescription: "A short, clean no, said once, without a justification tacked onto the end."
---
There is a sentence I have been practicing. It is short, it is simple, and it might be one of the hardest things I have ever tried to say without immediately following it with a justification. The sentence is: I am not doing that. Not "I'm not doing that because I'm busy." Not "I'm not doing that right now, but maybe later." Just the plain sentence, with nothing hanging off the end of it.

The first time I said it without a justification was on a call at work, six people on the line. A manager from another team asked me to join a cross-functional working group. He framed it like a compliment. "We think you'd be great for this." And I felt the old machinery start up inside me, the part of my brain that calculates how saying yes will make me look collaborative and visible and safe. But it was not on my scoreboard.

So I said, "Thanks, but I'm not going to do that."

The silence was excruciating. Maybe three seconds. It felt like thirty. He was waiting for the "because." I did not give him one.
