---
title: The Media Sabbath
date: 2023-06-11
pageDescription: "A weekly day without news or feeds, tried again each time it fails."
---
Pick one day a week and take everything off the menu: no news, no social media, no newsletters, no feeds. A full day with no one else's content arriving in your head.

The first time you try it, you probably will not make it past lunch. That is fine. It is not a failure. It is just data about how strong the habit is. Try again next week anyway.

"My first attempt lasted until 11 AM. I picked up my phone out of pure reflex, scrolled for ten seconds, then put it down. The second week I made it to 3 PM. By the third week I stopped counting and just noticed I'd had an entire Sunday thought that was actually mine."

That is the actual shape of this practice. Not a clean win on day one, but a slow climb where each week you last a little longer before reflex wins. The point was never perfection. It was noticing what shows up in your own head once nobody else's feed is filling the space first. Expect to fail partway through the first one, and try again anyway.
