---
title: "What Brain Fry Is"
date: 2023-01-15
pageDescription: "Brain fry is the specific exhaustion of constantly supervising and correcting AI generated work."
---
Brain fry is a specific kind of tired, not the ordinary kind that comes from a long day of hard work.

CNBC reported on the term in 2026, using it to describe the cognitive fatigue that comes from constantly managing, supervising, correcting, and switching between AI tools. It is not the fatigue of building something with your own hands or thinking through a hard problem start to finish. It is closer to the fatigue of standing at the end of a production line that runs faster than you can properly check, waving pieces through, catching what you can, knowing some of it is slipping past anyway.

You are not doing less work when a tool drafts something for you. You are doing a different kind of work, watching, verifying, correcting, and stitching results together across too many separate systems at once, and that kind of work is exhausting in its own particular way.

Naming it helps, even a little. Brain fry is not a sign that you are bad at using these tools. It is a sign that supervision itself, done constantly and at speed, is a real cost that nobody priced in when the tools first arrived.
