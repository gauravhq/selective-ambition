Most advice about slowing down quietly assumes you can afford to. Quit the job. Move to the country. Delete the apps. But if you have a mortgage, a family, or a career you are not ready to abandon, "just do less" can feel like a luxury written for someone else's life.

So start smaller. Not a clean break, a five per cent shift. One evening reclaimed. One meeting declined. One notification turned off for good. One project quietly set down so the others can breathe.

Five per cent sounds like nothing. It is not. For some people, protecting a single hour is the bravest thing they will do all year, precisely because it happens inside a life that does not make it easy.

JOMO is not only for people who can walk away. It is most needed by the people who cannot. You do not have to burn your life down to change it. You just have to move one thing, on purpose, and notice how the whole week feels different afterward.
