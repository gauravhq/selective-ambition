One distinction makes sense of a Tuesday like that: busy and stretched are not the same.

There was a month when I was genuinely busy: one big deadline, long focused days, the kind of tired where you fall asleep in ten seconds. When it passed, I rested, and the rest worked. Then came a stretch with no single deadline, just seven or eight medium-priority things running at once. I worked the same hours, maybe fewer, and felt far worse. The tiredness didn't live in my muscles. It lived behind my eyes.

Busy is a schedule problem, and schedules have fixes. Stretched is when you are running out not of hours but of yourself. Not too many hours. Too many selves. [Busy Versus Stretched](/blog/busy-versus-stretched/).
