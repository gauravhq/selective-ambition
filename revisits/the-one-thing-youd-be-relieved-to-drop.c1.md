When I ran my own list through one test, the first item was a podcast. I had carried the idea for over a year: a name, a rough format, a list of potential guests. The test was whether I would still want it if nobody ever knew, and the answer came fast. No. I didn't want to record a podcast. I wanted to have one.

What surprised me was what happened after I let it go. I expected it to nag at me. It didn't. It fell away quietly, as if it had been waiting for permission to leave. It had only ever been heavy because I was carrying it.

So if something came to mind when you read the question above, try that test on it. The relief is usually waiting on the other side of the answer. [The Nobody-Knew Test](/blog/the-nobody-knew-test/) goes further.
