My clearest one took three weeks. Marcus, someone I had worked with two years before, asked me to join a cross-team data migration. His email was generous. He had asked for me specifically. I sat on it for four days without replying, then spent almost three weeks drafting and deleting a four-sentence decline. I kept wanting to say no while making it sound like the universe was saying no for me.

When I finally sent it, I felt lighter. Physically lighter. Not immediately, it took about ten minutes, but it came: a wave of relief I wasn't expecting. I hadn't realised how much weight the deciding was adding until I put it down.

No is a short word that takes a long time to say. [The Three Types of No](/blog/the-three-types-of-no/) covers the other kinds.
