I did try saying mine out loud once, over dinner with a friend. I told him I thought I was doing too much and it was making me miserable. He took a sip of his beer and said, "So do less." When I tried to explain the guilt, he said I just needed to prioritise better.

I stopped talking about it after that. He was answering a logistics question and I was asking an identity question. I wasn't saying I had too many tasks. I was saying I didn't know who I was if I wasn't doing all of them.

So when you say yours out loud, notice which question you are really asking. If it is the identity one, a list of tips won't answer it, and that doesn't mean the stress isn't real. Naming it is still the first step. [The Guilt Loop](/blog/the-guilt-loop/).
