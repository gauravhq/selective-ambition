Here is one of mine. I once finished everything I needed to do by 3:30 on a Friday. Inbox cleared, deliverables sent. The sun was out and I could have gone for a walk. Instead I spent two hours rearranging a spreadsheet nobody had asked for, moving columns, adding colour to headers. At 5:45 I sent it to my manager with a note saying I had got ahead on it. He replied "great, thanks" and probably never opened it.

I had stayed visible until 5:45 on a Friday, and somehow that mattered more to me than the walk. I chose the performance over the afternoon and didn't even realise I was choosing.

That is the sorting this exercise is really about. Not lazy versus hardworking. Seen versus useful. [The Friday Spreadsheet](/blog/the-friday-spreadsheet/).
