The first time I tried a day like this, I lasted until about 11 in the morning. My hands reached for the phone before I had decided anything. I put it down, and twenty minutes later it was back in my hand.

By the third hour I had settled into a restless truce with the quiet. I sat on the balcony and watched traffic, something I hadn't done in years. My wife said I seemed fidgety. Later she said I seemed calmer. Both were true, just at different hours.

The third time, I made it the whole day. Around 4 PM, at the dining table with a cup of tea, an idea about my project arrived. I wrote it on the back of an electricity bill. It needed silence, and I hadn't given it any in months. More in [The Media Sabbath](/blog/the-media-sabbath/).
