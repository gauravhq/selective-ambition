Mine was reading. Novels, specifically: stories about people who don't exist, doing things that don't matter. I used to read like that every week, then stopped because it felt unproductive. When I started again, it felt like finding an old friend.

Then I tried drawing, badly, and enjoyed it anyway. I bought a cheap sketchbook and drew the blue mug with the chipped handle. My wife tilted her head and asked if it was a vase. We stood in the kitchen laughing until the laughter was feeding itself.

Drawing is the only thing I do where quality doesn't matter and nobody is waiting for the output. Washing dishes by hand is close: accomplishment always asks for more, and a clean pot asks for nothing. More in [Drawing the Mug Badly](/blog/drawing-the-mug-badly/).
