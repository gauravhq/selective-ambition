I used to say it with pride. I would list my schedule like an achievement: the day job, the evening project, the weekend learning, the idea for next month. People said "wow, that's a lot" and I heard admiration. I didn't hear the concern.

My mother heard it differently. She called one evening while I had her on speaker and kept working. She could hear the typing. She asked what I was doing, and I listed five things. She was quiet for a few seconds, then said, "You sound like your father before he got his blood pressure medication." I laughed it off. I thought about that sentence for weeks.

When busyness is your identity, slowing down doesn't feel like rest. It feels like erasing the only thing that made you visible. [You Sound Like Your Father](/blog/you-sound-like-your-father/).
