I tried this on a Sunday afternoon with a clean notebook and fresh coffee. I wrote the prompt and then nothing. I wrote something about a data platform and crossed it out. Then "getting better at leadership," which isn't even a project. Forty-five minutes later the coffee was cold and the page was full of crossed-out lines, as if my brain had a defense attorney for every idea I'd ever had.

When I did eventually pick one, I wrote "not now" next to the other nine. Within two weeks the one had moved further than it had in three months of trying to do everything. Three months on, I reread the nine. I couldn't remember why three of them had ever excited me.

The difficulty isn't a sign you're doing it wrong. It is the content. [One-Thing Seasons](/blog/one-thing-seasons/).
