I found my own answer by accident. I had just finished a big piece of work and wanted to tell someone. I scrolled my recent calls. The last real conversation, not a work call, not a quick request to pick up milk, was eleven days ago.

So I told the AI instead. It said it was a great accomplishment. It was the right response, and it was completely empty.

After that I started having one real conversation a week. One of them was a call to my friend Raj, with no reason and no agenda. The first five minutes were brutal. Then he told me his dad was sick. He had been carrying it alone for three weeks, simply because nobody had called. I listened for forty minutes. When we hung up he said he needed that. I needed it too. [The Raj Call](/blog/the-raj-call/) tells the rest.
